## Overview

The RR-DR60's speaker amplifier runs from the two AAA cells directly, through a switching transistor, while everything else runs from a regulated 3.3 V converter (A-013). In the model, fresh cells and tired cells sound alike until playback reaches the amplifier's clip ceiling, and there a tired pair clips earlier and lower: the model puts the end-of-life ceiling about 9 dB lower (A-056, low to medium confidence). Spec 009 modeled the amplifier at a nominal 2.8 V supply; this feature makes the supply a value the user sets, so a recording can be played as the model plays it with fresh or tired cells. It is a **unit condition**, like the hiss level: a property of the played unit, not a device control a hand moves, and not something the model drains on its own.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Play the unit on tired cells (Priority: P1)

As a user of the app, I set the battery voltage and hear loud playback through the speaker clip earlier and lower, while quiet playback is unchanged; as an app developer, I set it live (a plain setter or a scheduled change) and it moves over 20 ms without a click.

**Why this priority**: it is the feature; the product brief lists battery state among the live unit conditions of the free tier.

**Independent Test**: at the six law points and seven host rates, the below-knee stimuli are bit-identical to the default run; a tone 6 dB over the ceiling has a peak after stage 11 equal to b; a change lands on its position and is the splice of two constant runs after N samples.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the default (3.0 V cells), **When** any input is processed on either route, **Then** the output is bit-identical to 0.17.0.
2. **Given** a −30 dBFS 1 kHz tone and the −20 dBFS voiced stimulus at the reference volume position (stage 11's input below 0.85·b at every value in range), **When** the battery is set to any of the six law points, **Then** stage 11's output is bit-identical to the default run's.
3. **Given** a full-scale 1 kHz tone at `VOLUME_6DB_OVER` on the speaker route (hard-clipped at every value), **When** the battery is set to 2.96, 2.76, 2.66, 2.52, 2.42, 2.32 and 2.22 V, **Then** the peak after stage 11 (the AES17 amplitude after a 0.2 s settle, the stimulus at the peak phase) equals b within 1e-6 and reads −0.3, −1.9, −2.9, −4.4, −5.7, −7.3 and −9.1 dB re 1.0 within 0.05 dB (the register's trajectory); spec 009's knee table reproduces with the level axis shifted by 20·log10(b) and the 10 % THD input point reads +2.27 dB + 20·log10(b) within 0.1 dB at 44.1 kHz and above; the reference-position case (a tone at the nominal ceiling, clipped at a tired battery) is reported.
4. **Given** a scheduled change at output sample P, **When** the stream runs at block sizes 1, 64, 4096 and the irregular partition, **Then** the outputs and logs are identical, sample P is the first transition sample, sample P + N − 1 carries the new factor exactly (the splice of the two constant runs from there), transition sample ⌊N/4⌋ carries b₀ + 0.15625·Δb through the hook, and the click energy of the change on a hard-clipped tone is reported; a change on the earphone route is logged and has no effect.
5. **Given** the same stimulus on the earphone route or through the record path, **When** the battery is set to any value, **Then** the output is bit-identical to the default's.

### User Story 2 - The condition is described, kept and visible (Priority: P2)

As an app developer, I read the control manifest's entry (a unit condition, live, its range and default, one neutral sentence with the product rule), the value survives `reset` and `reconfigure` like the route, the meters tell me how often the amplifier clipped and at what ceiling, and the law and the alkaline trajectory are engine data my presets can follow.

**Independent Test**: the manifest entry's bounds equal the setter's acceptance; the value survives the lifecycle calls; the meters' clip count equals the count of stage 11 output samples at or above the knee's start in a twin at the tap; the published factor equals the engine's at every value; stage 15's note names the control.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a value set, **When** `reset` or `reconfigure` runs, **Then** the value is kept and the next output starts at that factor with no transition.
2. **Given** a value outside 2.2 … 3.0 V or not finite, **When** set or scheduled, **Then** it is refused with a distinct error and nothing changes.
3. **Given** the C API, **When** the setter, the getter, the schedule, the meters and the published law are used, **Then** the output, the entries and the figures equal the Rust path's bit for bit.
4. **Given** a hard-clipped tone at a non-default value, **When** a call is processed, **Then** the meters' amplifier-clip count equals the twin's count at the tap after stage 11 and the ceiling factor read-out equals b; at the default with below-knee material both rest (0 and 1).
5. **Given** the published trajectory, **When** each row's voltage is set, **Then** `ceiling_db` equals the register's dB figure within 0.05 dB and the engine's clipped peak equals `ceiling_factor`.

### Edge Cases

- **Stage 11 bypassed**: the value is accepted, logged and taken at once; no effect; the clip meter rests.
- **The earphone route**: no effect; a change is logged; a route change takes the factor at once and ends a transition in progress (the amplifier is in standby on the earphone route).
- **The one-pass's front taps and the taps before stage 11**: no effect, logged.
- **The output clamp** (spec 010) still bounds at ±1.0; the speaker route's provable bound is b × the template's L1 norm, so the clamp engages on clipped material above about 2.32 V and the clamp count changes with the value; a matrix row reports the route peak and the clamp count on a full-scale square at three values.
- **Two changes at one position**: the later wins, one transition.
- **A volume transition and a battery transition in flight at once**: independent states on stage 11, each along its own smoothstep; the clipper acts on the gain-applied sample.
- **A change scheduled past a playback's end** is dropped without an entry (spec 015's rule).
- **The meters** (spec 016): the route peak reads the lowered ceiling; the gain-reduction figure is the limiter's and does not move; the new clip count and factor join the playback group.
- **The lower bound**: 2.2 V is where the device would show its low-battery indicator and stop (S-001); the model plays it.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The engine MUST hold a battery value in volts of the cell pair, single precision, range 2.2 … 3.0 inclusive, default 3.0, set and read by a plain setter and getter and schedulable on the playback and one-pass streams through the existing schedule in the output clock, with spec 015's stream rules (each stream its own applied factor and entries; a plain setter lands on both streams; a scheduled change lands on its stream and becomes pending for the other; the getter reads the most recently landed value); kept through `reset` and `reconfigure`; a value out of range or not finite MUST be refused with a distinct error and leave the value unchanged.
- **FR-002**: The speaker amplifier's clipper MUST scale by b = (V − 1.8) ÷ 1.2 (exactly 1.0 at the default, pinned): the knee's start at 0.85·b, the hard ceiling at b, the parabola between them, the sample never scaled, so stage 11's output below the start is bit-identical to the default's at every value and the output scale stays 1.0 = the nominal ceiling.
- **FR-003**: A change MUST move the factor over the volume transition's length along its smoothstep from the factor last applied, with its own state (independent of a volume transition), landing on its position (the landing sample is transition sample 1, the target exact at sample N); it MUST be taken at once at a stream's first sample, while stage 11 is bypassed, while the earphone route is active, and at a route change.
- **FR-004**: The record path, the earphone route and every tap before stage 11 MUST be unaffected at every value.
- **FR-005**: At the default every output MUST be bit-identical to 0.17.0 (the twelve golden files, their SHA guards, every smoke hash); the feature MUST have its own golden file over the six law points, both routes, the clipped and the below-knee stimuli and the seven rates.
- **FR-006**: The figures MUST come from the design script's `--battery` print pinned by tests: the factor at the six law points and the register's seven trajectory rows (unrounded within 1e-9; the register's rounded table within 0.05 dB, which follows from the rounding); the knee table's shift and the 10 % THD point's move at 44.1 kHz and above.

### Key Entities

- **Battery value**: the cell pair's voltage, 2.2 … 3.0 V, default 3.0; an engine control (unit condition), kept through reset and reconfigure; single precision.
