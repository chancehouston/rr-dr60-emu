## Summary

A harness-only feature. One new checks module computes nine frame-level measures on the output of ten seeded stimulus rows through eight named configurations, under the harness's deterministic-math rules, and prints a canonical report whose text at three fixed rates is a golden. Six self-consistency checks assert; every other figure is reported. The guide gains a generated section. No library, audio, C, Swift or register change; no version bump.

## Research

- **R-01 The reader's math** (coach 3, 13; hardware B13, P11). `nonspeech_checks` is under the workspace's `disallowed-methods` with no `#![allow]` and calls no `analysis::` item (`us3_nonspeech::nonspeech_reader_uses_detmath_only`, a source scan). LPC by autocorrelation over the 120-sample frame (rectangular window; pre-emphasis 0.9 within the frame, the first sample against 0) and Levinson; the envelope on the 10 Hz grid as `1 / (c₀ + 2Σ c_m cos(mω))` from the LPC polynomial's autocorrelation `c_m`, so one cosine table `COS[g][m]` (g = 0 … 300, m = 0 … 8, `detmath::cos`) suffices; dB as `20·detmath::ln(x) ÷ LN10`, AES17 (`10·log10(2·mean square)` for the level); RMS and the autocorrelation's denominator via `detmath::sqrt`; the Hann window and the envelope DFT through cosine and sine tables indexed by `(k·n) mod N` for the cell's own N (`detmath::cos` / `sin`); medians sorted with `total_cmp`; no `HashMap`; "−0.000" normalised to "0.000"; printing by `format!("{:.3}")` (exact decimal rounding, platform-independent). Levels enter through `stimulus::amplitude` / `scale_to_level` (detmath).
- **R-02 The resampler to 6 kHz** (hardware P10). `to_device_rate(x, rate)`: the identity at 6 kHz; otherwise the windowed sinc of `analysis::resample_to`'s design (Hamming over 32 zero crossings each side at the lower rate, cut-off 2700 Hz) rebuilt with detmath: with the ratio `rate : 6000 = M : L` in lowest terms, the centre of output m is `(m·M) div L` with phase `(m·M) mod L`, so a table of `L = 6000 ÷ gcd(rate, 6000)` phases (20 at 44.1 kHz, 10 at 88.2 kHz, 3 at 8 and 16 kHz, 1 at 48 and 96 kHz) × the taps (473 at 44.1 kHz), built once per rate; about 7 × 10⁷ multiply-adds per cell at 96 kHz.
- **R-03 The configurations** (D3; hardware B5, P-review "keep"). Named in `configs::settings`, each with its tap in its doc comment: `nonspeech_c0` = `bypass_all`; `nonspeech_c1` = `default`; `nonspeech_c2` = `codec_only`; `nonspeech_c3` = `default_codec` with `no_agc`, `no_vas` (band, clamp, coder; `Tap::AfterPlayback`); `nonspeech_c4` = `Settings::new(rate)` with the gate off, the output chain's three stages off, `Tap::AfterPlayback` (the microphone, the limiter, the band, the clamp, the coder; bit-identical to `OutputSettings::BYPASSED`); `nonspeech_c5` = c4 with the gate on at level 3; `nonspeech_c6` = c5 with `vas.sensitivity = 5`, `mic.hiss_offset_db = 12.0`; `nonspeech_c7` = `Settings::new(rate)` (the route output at `VOLUME_REFERENCE`, 3.0 V; its speaker template 750 Hz / Q 1.8 is (h)'s resonator). Two identities in `nonspeech_matrix::nonspeech_configs`: C4 with the limiter off is bit-identical to C4 on every stimulus (below the knee: the reference's peak −9.4 dBFS against −1), and C4 with the microphone and limiter off is bit-identical to C3 (the base change `settings_007` → `Settings::new` proven to be one stage group).
- **R-04 The span and the alignment** (P4; coach 9; hardware B12, P7). Each cell is fed the stimulus (4096-sample calls) then exactly `L = latency_samples()` zeros; the output is the calls' returned samples (no drain); the span in output samples is `[settle + L, in_len + L)` with `settle = 0.5 s` (575 frames where nothing pauses); it is cut at the host rate with a guard of the FIR's half-span each side, resampled by R-02, the guard dropped, framed; in drop mode (C5, C7 on pausing stimuli) the output is shorter and the span ends at the output's end (kept output, said in the report). Splices: a splice resets every frame-to-frame state and measure (3)'s 100-sample history (zeroed) at the 6 kHz frame containing `⌊one_pass_splice_output_position(e) · 6000 ÷ rate⌋ − (the span's start at 6 kHz)` (spec 028's heard point; a reader unit pin). Measure (6) prints "—" whenever the span contains a splice or is under 90 % active; inactive frames enter the log-envelope series at the −75 dBFS floor. The pass-through check (D6 ii) reads the input over `[settle, in_len)` through the same reader: at 6 kHz the two sample sets are identical (L = 0, verified), so bit for bit; elsewhere the engine's own resampler pair and a sub-sample residue remain, bounded by R-05b.
- **R-05 The cross-rate spread** (D5, P6; coach 18; hardware P8). From the hardware review's probes with the D4 definitions (the `r_fix` column is measure (3)): periodicity C3 0.287 / 0.287 / 0.285 and C1 0.241 / 0.237 / 0.236 at 6 / 48 / 44.1 kHz, so (iv)'s C3 − C1 is +0.044 … +0.054 (bound +0.03 holds); the 2 – 8 Hz share on steady noise over active frames 0.24 – 0.31 across rates (realisation noise: each rate its own noise), so the share's tolerance will be about 0.1, not 0.05. The rule, applied after the first harness print and recorded in a dated Clarifications session: tolerance = 2 × the printed spread, floors 0.01 (periodicity, fractions), 0.1 (peaks), 0.1 (shares), 0.2 dB (flux), 1 dB (level), ±1 frame (counts); scope as P6; a regression guard, not a precision figure.
- **R-05b D6 ii's tolerances** (coach 4). Per continuous measure: 2 × max |C0 − input-own| over the stimuli at the measurement rates, floors 0.001 (fractions, periodicity), 0.1 Hz (movement), 0.01 dB (level, flux); counts ±1 frame; recorded in the same session before the bless; step 3 asserts (ii) bit for bit at 6 kHz and reports the other rates; step 5b asserts them, shown red with a doctored tolerance then green.
- **R-06 Cost and coverage** (P5; coach 16; hardware B4, P9). Ten stimulus rows × eight configurations = 80 cells per rate: 240 in the golden, 560 in the seven-rate matrix. Release about 0.3 – 0.5 s per cell (the coder's 0.78 µs per device sample plus the host-rate front and the reader); the golden about 2 min, the matrix about 4 min. Under instrumentation a coder cell costs about 3 – 4 s, so every binary computes only the cells it reads through a memo keyed by (stimulus, configuration, rate) and the golden is ignored there; the budget and the record as P5.
