## Decisions for sign-off

The owner reads this section first. Each decision gives the recommendation; the alternatives are in the Assumptions section. The owner's three choices (the first component, the insertion point, one slot) are built in; the three draft reviews are applied at their defaults and listed under Clarifications. The term is **component** throughout ("kind" is the control manifest's field for a control's class); "type" is the filter's own parameter.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-11-01 (the owner's choices before the draft)

- The first component is a **band filter** (a reverb and a three-band equalizer were the alternatives); the only insertion point in 1.0 is **before the limiter on the record side** (before the voice gate and before the output chain were offered); **one slot per point** (an ordered chain was the alternative).

### Session 2026-11-01 (the draft reviews: coach 1 – 16, hardware B1 – B11, product M1 – M7)

- **The section form** (hardware B1): a direct-form biquad with per-sample coefficient recompute overshoots the frozen response by up to +13.8 dB on a downward frequency move (a simulation on the 48 kHz notch 21.6 kHz → 20 Hz with a 100 Hz tone; +12.0 dB band-pass, +8.9 dB low-pass); the topology-preserving state-variable form stays within +3.6 dB. D4 chose the state-variable form; the harness reproduces the simulation as a reported row (spec 019's lesson: a reviewer's figure is reproduced before the records quote it); the +3.6 dB excess is a reported figure, not a floor.
- **A type change blends** (coach 1): two sections per slot, a 20 ms blend from P; the mutant `type_instant` (an instant switch with the state kept) is the click reference.
- **The record form moves to version 2** (coach 2); the 0.24 reader reads both versions.
- **The cost budget is 3 % solo, 5 % on the runner** (coach 3): a 1 % gate is below the house A/B's two-point spread (spec 017's lesson).
- **The parameter store persists** (coach 4, hardware B3): the draft's D4 ("the defaults are the state a slot holds the moment the component is set") contradicted the edge case; the store is created at construction and kept; `set_component` clears the filter state only.
- **The clamp is applied, not refused** (coach 5, hardware B9): the published range stays 20 – 20 000 Hz; the engine applies 0.45 × the rate; the record stores the requested value; the ceiling is a published function, not a per-rate control range.
- **The response oracle is a design script** (coach 6, hardware B4, B5): `tools/band-filter/design_band_filter.py` prints the digital response and the coefficients (`--check`, `--write` byte-identical); the harness reads the impulse response at `AfterInsert` by the DTFT at exact frequencies in a configuration with stages 1 – 2 and the limiter bypassed (the impulse's length at least 8 Q / (π f) + 0.1 s); a separate **placement identity** in the default engine (the run's `AfterInsert` equals the harness's f64 section applied to the twin's `AfterMicAmp`, bit for bit, same seed and counter) proves where the slot sits with the hiss and stage 2 on; the DC/Nyquist table: low-pass 1·g at DC and 0 at Nyquist, high-pass 0 and 1·g, band-pass 0 and 0, notch 1·g and 1·g, the zeros exact and the ones within 1e-10 in the coefficients.

- **The store is the cross-stream carrier** (coach 8); a component set is never an entry; `component_parameter_value` added (coach 9); the determinism and real-time FR added (coach 10); the header line dropped (coach 11); "component", never "kind" (coach 12); the vague words struck (coach 13); the golden about 80 entries (coach 14); the manifest sentences drafted (coach 15, product M3); the mutants, the baseline figures, the re-bless list, the C smoke case, the A/B mode and the `Error` variants named (coach 16).

#### The manifest sentences (the wording scan applies)

- The insertion point: "Between stage 2, the microphone amplifier, and stage 3, the limiter, at the host rate; an addition of the app, the original recorder has no place for one."
- The component: "One second-order filter, low-pass, high-pass, band-pass or notch, at a chosen frequency and Q, then a gain of −24 to +24 dB; an addition of the app, not a part of the original recorder."
- `insert.before_limiter`: "Puts one of the engine's own components, or none, into the slot after the microphone amplifier and before the limiter; an addition of the app, not a part of the recorder."
- `band_filter.type`: "Chooses the band filter's shape: low-pass, high-pass, band-pass or notch."
- `band_filter.frequency_hz`: "Sets the band filter's corner or centre frequency; above 0.45 × the host rate the engine applies 0.45 × the host rate."
- `band_filter.q`: "Sets how sharp the band filter's corner is, or how narrow its peak or notch; at the top of the range the corner peaks by about twenty decibels."
- `band_filter.gain_db`: "Sets the level after the band filter's section; a large gain drives the limiter and keeps the recorder from pausing."
- The app card (product M3): "Band filter (added). Shapes what the recorder hears before its limiter: one low-pass, high-pass, band-pass or notch section at the chosen frequency and Q, followed by a gain. Because it sits before the limiter and the voice-activated pause, it also changes how hard the limiter works and when the recorder pauses. The original recorder has no such filter."

## Overview

The engine's signal chain is fixed: fifteen stages in the device's order, each one modeled on the recorder's documentation or registered as an assumption, bypassable but never inserted, moved or removed (brief.md 277). The product direction since 2026-10-15 is that paid expansion adds components to the back-panel workbench, and that the places a component can go must come from the engine, not the app (brief.md 75). This feature builds the mechanism and ships its first component free: a **slot** at one published **insertion point**, before the limiter on the record side, that can hold one of the engine's own **components**, and a **band filter** as the first component. A component in the record path changes what the recorder hears and therefore what it stores; the processing record carries the component and its parameter changes so a session can be reproduced. Every description says what the component is: an addition of the app, not a part of the original recorder; it describes what the filter does to a tone, never what a listener might find.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - A filter in the chain (Priority: P1)

An app user opens the workbench, puts the band filter into the slot before the limiter, hears the recorder record through it, adjusts its frequency while recording, and takes it out again.

**Independent Test**: set the component, record an impulse with each filter type in the oracle configuration, read the response against the design script, schedule a frequency change and a type change and read the transition and the blend, set the component back to none and compare with a run that never had it.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the oracle configuration (stages 1 – 2 and the limiter bypassed, the filter in the slot, the tap after the slot), **When** an impulse is recorded, **Then** the response read by the DTFT at exact frequencies equals the design script's within 0.1 dB and 2° from 20 Hz to 0.45 × the rate at every rate, the structural zeros read ≤ −120 dB and the unity points within 1e-6 dB; **given** the default engine, the run's tap after the slot equals the harness's section applied to the empty-slot twin's tap after the amplifier, bit for bit (the placement identity).
2. **Given** a recording in progress with the filter in the slot, **When** a frequency change is scheduled at input sample P, **Then** the parameter follows the 20 ms smoothstep from P in log-frequency (within 1e-9 of the formula on the trace), the entry is logged at P with its tag, and on the asserted rows (a ±12 dB gain step with the tone away from the frequency; a one-octave frequency move at Q 0.7071) the click reader reads the transition at least 30 dB below the instant-step mutant; the full-range moves, Q 10 and the state-variable form's transient excess are reported; **given** a type change at P, the two sections blend over 20 ms, the `type_instant` mutant the reference.
3. **Given** the slot set to none, **When** any stimulus is recorded or processed in one pass, **Then** the output is bit-identical to 0.23's at every rate and configuration (the sixteen golden guards and every smoke hash unmoved; the empty-slot twin bit-identical); **given** filter → none → filter, the run equals a run that first set the filter.
4. **Given** a recording or a playback in progress, **When** `set_component` is called, **Then** it is refused with `ComponentWhileActive` and nothing changes; **given** an idle engine, the component is set without allocating, is kept by `reset` and `reconfigure`, is read back, and a parameter landed before it is in force (the store).
5. **Given** the filter with the gain at +24 dB on a loud stimulus, **When** recorded, **Then** the limiter, the clamp and the gate respond as to any loud input (the limiter's dead time, the clamp's count); **given** a quiet recording, the gain at which the gate stops pausing is read per type at levels 3 and 5 (about +15 dB with the default threshold) and reported in the guide.

### User Story 2 - The engine describes the slot (Priority: P2)

An app developer builds the workbench's "add component" from the engine's data and reproduces a session.

**Independent Test**: read the insertion-point list, the component manifest and the control manifest against the engine; write and read a processing record with the filter; run the C parity rows and the promise script.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

2. **Given** the control manifest, **When** read, **Then** the five entries sit at indices 31 – 35 with the stated kinds, timings and sentences; `MAX_CHANGES_PER_CALL` is 146 by the restated derivation and a 142-entry buffer receives the first 142; the registers cross-check passes after one re-bless.

### User Story 3 - Nothing else moved (Priority: P3)

1. **Given** the A/B against d6cf258, **When** run solo and on the runner, **Then** the record path and the one-pass with the slot empty are within 3 % (5 % on the runner); the filter's cost and a transition's worst block are reported, the block within real time at 96 kHz.
2. **Given** the partition identities, **When** a recording with scheduled parameter changes is run at block sizes 1, 64, 4096 and irregular, **Then** the outputs and logs are identical.
3. **Given** the determinism and allocation-free suites, **When** extended by the slot, **Then** they pass; the slot block's growth inside the memory is a reported figure and the stored form is unmoved.

### Edge Cases

- A parameter change scheduled while the slot is empty: logged as an entry and landed in the store; a component set later starts from the store.
- A frequency above 0.45 × the host rate: accepted, the ceiling applied, the requested value stored and recorded; `reconfigure` to another rate re-applies the ceiling.
- A transition interrupted by a second landing within 20 ms: restarts from the parameter's current value (spec 011's rule); a type change during a frequency transition blends the sections while the trajectory continues.
- A component set to the component already in the slot: no change (and no entry, as never).
- `reconfigure`: the slot's component and store kept, the section rebuilt for the new rate, a running transition ended at its target.
- The one-pass and the recorder share the store (D3); a landing on one stream is pending for the other.
- The notch at a frequency a stored tone sits on: the tone attenuated by the design's depth, reported.
- A component in the slot during the stop's flush: the flush runs through it like the input does.
- The filter's state persists across recordings like the amplifier's; cleared by `reset`, `reconfigure` and `set_component`.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The engine MUST publish its insertion points and its components as constant data (D1, D2), one point and one component in 1.0, each entry with the fields of D1 and D2.
- **FR-002**: The slot MUST be empty by default, and an empty slot MUST leave every output bit-identical to 0.23 (the sixteen golden guards and every smoke hash unmoved).
- **FR-003**: The slot's component MUST be settable only while the engine is idle, refused otherwise with `Error::ComponentWhileActive`, never a change entry, kept by `reset` and `reconfigure`, readable back, and MUST never allocate; setting it MUST clear the component's filter state and keep the parameter store (D3).
- **FR-004**: The band filter MUST implement D4: the state-variable section, four types, the three continuous parameters with their published ranges and defaults, the applied frequency ceiling with its published function, the response within 0.1 dB and 2° of the design script at the seven rates, the structural zeros and unity points as stated.
- **FR-005**: The four parameters MUST be live on the record stream and the one-pass in the input clock through the schedule only, landing at P with the 20 ms smoothstep of D3 (a type landing a 20 ms blend), every landing a change entry, the store the cross-stream carrier, `component_parameter_value` reading the store; the playback stream MUST refuse them with `ControlNotOnStream`; out-of-range values MUST be refused with `InvalidControlValue`.
- **FR-006**: The control manifest MUST append the five entries of D5 at indices 31 – 35 with no existing index moving; `MAX_CHANGES_PER_CALL` MUST be 146 by the restated derivation with the truncation rule documented.
- **FR-007**: `Tap::AfterInsert` MUST be appended at value 11 and MUST equal `AfterMicAmp` with the slot empty.
- **FR-008**: The processing record MUST move to form version 2 as D7 states, the reader MUST read versions 1 and 2, the re-render MUST reproduce the digest with a component in the slot, the named deltas MUST fail it, and an unknown component MUST be flagged and refused.
- **FR-009**: The C API MUST gain the functions, defines, structs and statuses of D9 with no change to any existing struct; the promise script MUST pass with both 12a3c45 and d6cf258 in CI; the Swift re-render MUST carry the component.
- **FR-010**: The empty slot's cost MUST be within 3 % of d6cf258 on the record path and the one-pass (5 % on the runner); the filter's cost and a transition's per-sample work MUST be reported and the worst 64-sample block asserted within real time at 96 kHz.
- **FR-011**: Every text describing the slot or the filter MUST call it an addition of the app and describe what it does to a tone (D11); the wording denylist applies to every sentence the cross-check reads.
- **FR-012**: The slot and the filter MUST be deterministic (`detmath` only, bit-identical across the supported platforms), partition-independent, and allocation-free after construction (both sections, the store and the transition state reserved).
- **FR-013**: The slot MUST add no latency with any 1.0 component (`latency_samples` 0); a component with latency is a later decision.

### Out of Scope

- An ordered chain per point; insertion points on the playback side or before the voice gate (later specs: the point list is the extension seam).
- Third-party or scripted components; reverb, equalization, flanging (later components on the same mechanism).
- A component inside the memory or the stored form; a component on the playback path; an entitlement or a price in the engine.

### Key Entities

- **Insertion point**: a published place in the chain where one slot sits (`before_limiter`).
- **Slot**: holds zero or one component; its component set while idle; its parameters live through the store.
- **Component**: an engine-owned processor with a manifest entry (`band_filter`).
- **Component manifest**: constant data describing every component and its parameters, latency and paths.
- **Parameter store**: the four values, created at the defaults, persisting, the cross-stream carrier.
- **Parameter transition**: the 20 ms smoothstep a landing follows in the parameter's own scale; a type landing's two-section blend.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

## Assumptions

- **Alternatives to D1**: several points at once (before the voice gate, before the output chain, before the limiter); rejected for 1.0 to keep one contract, with the list as the seam.
- **Alternatives to D2**: an open plug-in interface (rejected: no third-party code in the core, determinism and real-time safety could not be guaranteed); a cost class per component (deferred to the second component).
- **Alternatives to D3**: the component as a setting (rejected: it would grow the settings struct and move the ABI version under spec 022 D13); the component live (rejected: a component change mid-recording would need its own transition and a record rule); a plain setter for the parameters (rejected: spec 017's schedule-only rule for record-side values); an instant type switch with the state kept (the mutant and the click reference).
- **Alternatives to D4**: a direct-form biquad with per-sample recompute (rejected by the simulation of hardware B1: up to +14 dB over the frozen response on a downward move); a two-filter crossfade on direct forms (bounded, but it abandons the parameter-trajectory contract); a one-pole filter (cheaper, no Q); a fourth-order section (a design of its own); a gain before the section (rejected: a post-gain keeps the section's numeric range); the constant-skirt band-pass (rejected: its peak grows with Q beyond the gain parameter).
- **Alternatives to the transition**: an instant coefficient step (a click on content near the frequency; the mutant and the reference); coefficient interpolation (stable, since the stability region is convex, but it does not follow the stated log-frequency trajectory).
- **Alternatives to D5's 146**: holding 142 by refusing a fifth pending mirror (rejected: a silent drop).
- **Alternatives to D7**: optional lines under form version 1 (rejected: a version-1 reader would call the record malformed rather than newer).
- **Alternatives to D8**: a 1 % budget (rejected: below the A/B's noise).
- **Alternatives to D11**: the filter in a paid package (rejected by the owner: one basic effect ships free); a tier keyed by the controls' kind (rejected: emulator options are Workbench-only, which would lock the free filter); an entitlement in the engine (rejected: commerce is the app's).
- **The gain range**: ±12 dB would keep the default gate pausing under every setting; ±24 dB kept with the interaction documented (the owner's default).
