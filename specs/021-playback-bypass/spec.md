## Overview

Every playback-side stage of the engine can be bypassed today, but only by `reconfigure`, which rebuilds the engine and resets every path: an A/B of "with the speaker" against "without the speaker" on the same passage means stop, change, play again, and remember. The Workbench wants the switch while the recording plays, so that the listener hears the stage's contribution appear and disappear on the same syllable. A switch in one sample would click (a stage's output and its input differ by the stage's whole effect: a filter's phase, an amplifier's gain and clipping, a speaker template's resonance), so the switch is a short crossfade between the stage's output and its input, in the engine's one transition length of 20 ms (specs 011, 017, 018, 019). The stage keeps running while bypassed so that switching it back in is seamless, and nothing else about the engine moves: a constant state is bit-identical to 0.20, the player's latency, drain and length are unchanged, and the record path is untouched.

The four switches become live values in the pattern of spec 017's record-side values: the setting itself is scheduled on a stream, lands on a sample, is logged, and is reflected by `settings()`. A stage that does not run on the stream cannot be switched on live, because its settle would have to be carried in the drain and its latency in the reported figure; this keeps every figure the app already relies on fixed, and the Workbench's A/B runs on an engine with every stage on.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Hear a stage come and go without a click (Priority: P1)

A Workbench listener plays a recording and switches the speaker template out and back in, then the amplifier, then the earphone output on the earphone route, then the codec output filter, each while the passage plays. Each switch is heard as the stage's effect fading over 20 ms, with no click, and the stage's effect is exactly present or exactly absent outside the fade.

**Why this priority**: it is the whole feature; everything else bounds it.

**Independent Test**: a stored −6 dBFS 1 kHz tone played on the speaker route with stage 12 switched off at a known position and on again later: before the landing the output equals the all-on run; from the blend's last sample it equals the same engine configured with stage 12 off over that twin's length; each blend sample equals y + w·(x − y) on a trace of stage 12's output and input; the click energy around the switch is at least 30 dB below the step's.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** any pre-021 configuration (every stage at its construction value, no bypass scheduled), **When** played, recorded and run one-pass at all seven host rates, **Then** every output sample is bit-identical to 0.20's: the thirteen audio golden guards, the record golden and every smoke hash unmoved.
2. **Given** a recording playing with every stage on and a change of stage k ∈ {11, 12, 13} to bypassed landing at host sample P, **When** the output is read, **Then** before P it equals the all-on run; blend samples n = 1 … N equal y + w_n·(x − y) of the stage's processed output and input with w(N/2) = 0.5 exactly and sample N the input selected; from P + N − 1 on it equals the same engine configured with stage k off, bit for bit over the twin's length, on the route the stage runs on (13 on the earphone route, 11 – 12 on the speaker route; stage 11 also on the earphone route), at the measurement rates; the live engine's remaining drain samples are reported by their peak.
3. **Given** stage 10 switched to bypassed on the playback stream at position P (landing at d(P)), **When** a device-rate trace of stage 10's output is read, **Then** before d(P) it equals the all-on device stream; blend samples 1 … 120 equal y + w·(x − y) with sample 120 the input selected; from d(P) + 119 on it equals the configured-off engine's device stream over its length; on the host output the identity holds from P + ⌈119·rate ÷ 6000⌉ + 2D (the harness measures the first differing and the first identical host sample and reports both); at 6 kHz the host output is the device stream.
4. **Given** a stage bypassed and a change back to on landing later, **When** read, **Then** the blend runs w from 1 to 0 by the same curve and from its last sample (the processed output selected) the output equals the all-on run with the same route, volume and battery schedule, bit for bit (the stage and its transitions ran warm throughout).
5. **Given** the stimuli of FR-007's table at 6 kHz in the stage-only configurations, **When** each stage is switched by the engine and by a reference that steps the weight, **Then** the crossfade's click energy under the reader is at least 30 dB below the step's on every asserted row; the reported rows (the level-change rows of stage 11 in dB, the 48 kHz rows, the voiced stimulus, the reversal) are printed.
6. **Given** the same schedule at block sizes 1, 64 and 4096 and irregular partitions, **When** played, **Then** the outputs and the logs are identical; the block-size-1 oracle of spec 015 holds.
7. **Given** a toggle back while a blend is at phase u = 1/2, **When** read, **Then** the weight returns along the reversed curve and reaches 0 within N/2 samples, to the bit against the reference; every sample lies within one ULP of the interval between the two branches.

### User Story 2 - The switch is bounded, described and safe (Priority: P2)

An app developer relies on the switch doing nothing else: the latency, drain and length fixed, a stage that does not run refused, every landing logged, the meters telling what is heard, the control manifest saying which controls are live, the level change of stage 11 published, and the cost within budget.

**Independent Test**: an engine configured with stage 12 off refuses a scheduled change to stage 12 on with the new status and plays bit-identically to 0.20; an engine with every stage on plays the same number of samples with any schedule as without; the log holds one entry per landing, chained, with the stream word; a landing on the one-pass becomes pending on the player.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an engine whose setting for stage k is off, or a one-pass whose configured tap does not reach stage k, **When** a change switching stage k on is scheduled, **Then** it is refused with `StageNotConfigured` (status 36), nothing is pending, and the output is bit-identical to 0.20; a value other than exactly 0 or 1 (a non-finite value included) is refused as an invalid control value; stage 10 on the one-pass and any of the four on the record stream are refused as control not on stream.
2. **Given** any schedule of the four switches on an engine with every stage on, **When** played to the end, **Then** `playback_latency_samples()`, the drain and the total played length equal the no-schedule figures at every rate; the one-pass's latency is unchanged.
3. **Given** changes landing on both streams, **When** the logs are read, **Then** each landing is one entry (position, control index, before, after as 0 or 1, the caller's tag, the stream word), chained (each before equals the previous after), and `settings()` reads the most recently landed value; a change to the value in force is a no-op entry; two changes of one stage at one position both apply and both log in submission order (no blend for off-then-on).
4. **Given** stage 12 landed bypassed on the one-pass, **When** a playback is started and runs, **Then** the player's first output adopts the value without an entry (spec 017 D4) and a landing on the player during a one-pass call becomes pending for the one-pass at its next sample with the same tag, logged there.
5. **Given** stage 11 bypassed on the speaker route with a tone above the knee, **When** the meters are read per block, **Then** `amp_clip_count` is 0 in blocks wholly at w = 1 and counts again from the first sample at w < 1; the route peak reads the blended output.
6. **Given** the control manifest, **When** read, **Then** the four entries read timing **live**, their paths, kinds, values and sentences unchanged, the canonical text re-blessed once; the stage manifest moved in its version line only; no new assumption.
7. **Given** the cost A/B against 0.20 in the all-on state with no landing, **When** run solo, **Then** the per-sample cost is within 3 % on the playback stream and the one-pass (5 % on the runner); the bypassed steady state, a blend in progress and the worst block are reported within the real-time budget; no call allocates.
8. **Given** the C API, **When** built, **Then** the header differs by the appended status and the changes-per-call bound only, the parity test shows the C path landing and blending exactly as the Rust path, and the C smoke gains a bypass case.
9. **Given** a live engine with stage 12 bypassed, **When** `reconfigure(settings())` is called and a change to stage 12 on is scheduled, **Then** it is refused (the trap the guide documents), while `reconfigure` with the stage on followed by a bypass at position 0 plays as the live bypass did.

### Edge Cases

- A change scheduled at position 0 of the playback stream (or placed between `start_playback` and the first play call) lands on the first output: spec 011's first-sample rule applies (the stage is switched at once, no blend, nothing has sounded yet); the entry is logged.
- Two changes of one stage at the same position are both applied before sample P and both logged in submission order (spec 015 AS7); the second reverses the first at phase 0, so off-then-on at one position produces no blend and two entries, `settings()` at the second's value.
- Changes of two different stages at the same position: both land; two independent blends run.
- A toggle back exactly at the blend's last sample: the new blend starts from w = 1 (the direction reversed at the end of the curve).
- Several toggles within one blend: each reverses the direction from the current phase; 0 ≤ w ≤ 1 and monotone within each direction; the output within one ULP of the two branches' interval.
- A playback stopped during a blend: the next playback starts at the landed values with no blend (a stream's first sample); the pending schedule is cleared with the playback; the landed values persist.
- `reset` re-applies `settings()` (the landed values) at once and clears the schedule and the log; `reconfigure(s)` takes `s` (spec 017).
- A bypass landing during the drain (past the recording's own part): the blend runs on the drain's samples; nothing is added past the drain.
- A stage not on the active route (12 on the earphone route, 13 on the speaker route): the change lands, logs and is inaudible; its blend's phase advances on every host sample regardless of the route, so the blend is over when a later route change makes the stage audible (the route change is itself logged); the app greys the inactive route's toggle.
- A route change during a blend resets the chain's sections as spec 015 does and keeps the weight and direction; the all-on oracle afterwards is the all-on run with the same route schedule.
- Stage 11 bypassed (D11): the volume position and the battery value act on the warm branch only, with their own 20 ms transitions exactly as if heard (spec 015's at-once rule is for a stage configured off); switching the stage back in hears the settled new value. The bypassed branch is the stage's input at unity: the level change by route and position is the guide's table (earphone +19.2 dB at the reference position, 0 dB at position 1; speaker −21.5 dB at position 1, about 0 dB at the reference position; position 0 silence against full scale).
- Stage 10 bypassed at a host rate other than 6 kHz: the blend is 120 device samples before the upsampler; the host output first differs bit for bit at P + 1 … 16 by rate (spec 015's speed-onset lesson), the audible onset is about D host samples after P (about 5 ms at 48 kHz, later than stages 11 – 13's at P), and the identity holds from P + ⌈119·rate ÷ 6000⌉ + 2D; the guide says an app may schedule stage 10 at P − D to hear it together with the others.
- The one-pass with the codec on: stages 11 – 13 land at the one-pass's apply point; the codec is untouched; stage 10 is refused there (D6).
- The one-pass at a tap before the chain, or at a tap before stage 10: the stages the tap does not reach are refused (D4); a tap the chain runs for behaves as the playback stream.
- A change scheduled on the record stream for any of the four: refused (control not on stream).

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The four playback-side stage switches (stage 10 `playback_stage_enabled`; stages 11 – 13 `output.volume_amp_enabled`, `output.speaker_enabled`, `output.earphone_enabled`) MUST be schedulable as live values through the existing scheduling call by their control-manifest indices, the value exactly 0 or 1 (refused otherwise, nothing stored): stages 11 – 13 on the playback stream and the one-pass stream, stage 10 on the playback stream only; landing at the top of host sample P (stage 10 at the speed's device-rate landing d(P)), logged as change entries (before and after 0 or 1, the stream word), reflected by `settings()` as the most recently landed value, with no plain setter; a switch of stages 11 – 13 landed on one stream MUST become pending for the other at its next sample (spec 017 D4; the player adopts at `start_playback` without an entry); the record stream MUST refuse all four and the one-pass stage 10 (control not on stream). The pending schedule follows spec 015's lifecycle; the landed value persists (`reset` re-applies `settings()`, `reconfigure(s)` takes `s`).
- **FR-002**: A landing MUST start a crossfade of N = 0.02 × rate host samples (120 device samples for stage 10) between the stage's processed output y and its input x: the stage's contribution is y + w_n·(x − y), w_n = 3u² − 2u³ at u = n ÷ N, n = 1 … N, the bypass weight running from 0 (on) to 1 (bypassed) or back; outside a blend and on its last sample the target branch MUST be selected, not computed; the downstream stages and the per-stage taps MUST receive the blended signal.
- **FR-003**: A stage configured on MUST keep running while bypassed (its state and its own transitions warm, the per-sample work unchanged), so that from the blend's last sample a bypass is bit-identical to the same engine configured with that stage off over the twin's own length, and a return is bit-identical to the all-on run with the same route, volume and battery schedule; for stage 10 the identities hold on the device-rate stream and on the host output from P + ⌈(N − 1)·rate ÷ 6000⌉ + 2D (measured).
- **FR-004**: A change switching on a stage that does not run on the stream (its construction setting off, or the one-pass's configured tap not reaching it) MUST be refused at scheduling with a new status, `StageNotConfigured` (36), whose text names the fix; the player's reported latency, drain and total length and the one-pass's latency MUST NOT depend on any live value (the live engine's length is the all-on length, longer than the configured-off twin's).

### Out of Scope

- Live bypasses of record-side stages (1 – 5, 7): the offline re-render through spec 020's processing record.
- The speech codec (fixes the memory's encoding and the latency), the speed stage (specs 013, 019) and the output clamp (D8).
- A stage's internal parameters (the volume position and the battery value are already live; the others are settings).
- Any new stage, any change to a stage's coefficients, pitch-kept speed (the next spec).
- Switching on a stage that does not run on the stream (D4; reconsider with a drain sized for every stage if the app asks); stage 10 on the one-pass (D6).
- A level-kept bypass of stage 11 (D11's alternative) unless the owner chooses it.

### Key Entities

- **Stage switch**: one of the four playback-side settings, a live value on its streams, logged by its control index.
- **Blend**: the N samples after a landing, each y + w·(x − y) of the stage's output and input; its phase u and direction are the per-stage state; w = 0 is "on", w = 1 "bypassed".
- **Warm stage**: a stage configured on whose filter and transitions run regardless of its live value; only its contribution to the signal is blended.
- **Neutral position**: the volume position per route at which stage 11's bypass changes no level (speaker: the reference position; earphone: position 1).

## Assumptions

- **Alternatives considered**: stages 11 – 13 only (D1: stage 10 is cheap and completes the set; its audibility is mainly at 6 kHz); a separate per-stage "bypass" control beside the setting (D2: two sources of truth for one fact; spec 017's pattern keeps one; its one argument, that `settings()` would never write a bypass back into a construction recipe, is answered by the guide's contract); two whole-chain branches with and without the toggled stage, blended at the route output (D3: twice the chain's work for 20 ms, identical for a single toggle, and ill-defined for overlapping toggles of two stages; the per-stage blend composes); allowing on-from-off with a drain sized for every stage (D4: a length change for every chain-off configuration, moving goldens; and the reported latency would have to be the all-on figure for a configured-off engine); waiting for a blend's end or restarting from rest on a toggle (D5: a two-state switch has a natural reverse; a wait would delay the listener's gesture by up to 20 ms, a restart would jump); stage 10 live on the one-pass through a device-clock apply point (D6: a new mechanism for a stage whose bypass is barely audible at the conversion rates); counting the warm knee's clips regardless (D7: the inside would show clipping nobody hears); a fifth live switch for the clamp (D8: a sample map with no state; later if asked); the level-kept bypass of stage 11 (D11: the product reviewer's recommendation; recorded with its costs for the owner's choice).
- **Not a device behavior**: the RR-DR60 has no stage switches; the crossfade and the warm stage are the emulator's conventions, stated as such in the guide and the CHANGELOG; the control manifest's sentences keep describing the default configuration (spec 014's rule); no assumption is registered or amended.
- **The 20 ms length** is the engine's one transition length (A-045 for the volume, spec 017's hiss, spec 018's battery, spec 019's speed), restated as a convention here; no new constant is published (a single transition constant for every engine transition is a pre-1.0 housekeeping candidate).
- **The taps read the blended signal** (`AfterPlayback`, `AfterVolumeAmp`, `AfterSpeaker`, `AfterEarphone`): what the next stage receives is what a tap shows.
- **Stage 10's audible onset differs** from stages 11 – 13's by the resampler's delay D at the conversion rates; documented, not compensated by the engine (an app may schedule it earlier).
- **The app** uses a toggle per stage, one change per gesture, in the order 12, 11, 13, 10, greys the inactive route's toggle, shows stage 11's level delta from the published gain-law functions, and offers a whole-output-path switch built from the per-stage switches; no new read-out is needed (the log gives the position and the manifest the timing).
