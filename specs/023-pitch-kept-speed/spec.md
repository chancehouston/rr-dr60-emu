### Session 2026-10-31 (the plan reviews: coach 1 – 16, hardware B1 – B8, product M1 – M9; dated restatements)

- **The pitch-kept tail is at most 120 device samples, in [60, 119]** (D4, FR-004 said ≤ 160): a grid bound, provable because the search window (40) is shorter than the hop (60), so a frame past the recording's end can carry content into its first half only; the simulation found no exceedance in 1500 random trials with the search allowed past K. ## Overview

Since 0.13 the engine plays a recording at the device's five speeds, the pitch moving with the speed, because the modeled mechanism is a resampling of the decoded stream. A listener who slows a playback to follow a passage hears the voice about nine semitones lower at level 1; this option keeps the pitch while changing the speed. The device's own mechanism is unknown (A-054); the research leans weakly toward a pitch-keeping device, of a class this option does not model. The feature adds the pitch-kept behavior as a selectable option beside the modeled default, as one more live control with the rules the speed level already has, without changing what the default engine sounds like. Every description of it says what it is: an engine convention, a time-scaling of the decoded stream, with artifacts of its own; neither mode adds anything to a recording or removes anything from it beyond those artifacts.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Slower, same pitch (Priority: P1)

A listener plays a recording at a slow level with the pitch-kept mode on and hears the voice at its own pitch, stretched; at a fast level, compressed; at level 3, unchanged.

**Independent Test**: play the stored tones, the vowel, the glide and the click train at each level in each mode; read the frequency, the level, the spurious products, the pitch tracks, the click counts, the length and the place.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the stored 1234.5 Hz tone in pitch-kept mode, **When** played at levels 1, 2, 4 and 5, **Then** the fundamental is within ±1 % of the stored one at every rate, the level within ±0.5 dB, and the spurious products at least 28 dB down at 6 kHz; the 1 kHz row reads the same frequency and level and sits at the floor; 2345 Hz and the two-tone row are printed.
2. **Given** the same tones in the default mode, **When** played at each level, **Then** the fundamental is the stored one times the ratio (within ±1 %) and the output equals 0.22's bit for bit.
3. **Given** the steady vowel in pitch-kept mode, **When** played at levels 1 and 5, **Then** its pitch is within ±1 % of the stored vowel's by the reader, whose own error on the stored stream is printed and smaller; **given** the glide, the median error within ±1 % and the p95 within ±2.5 % against the stored track at r·t, the max printed.
4. **Given** either mode at level 3, **When** played, **Then** the audio and every play report are bit-identical to 0.22 (a run without a mode change).
5. **Given** a playback in pitch-kept mode, **When** `place` is read after each call, **Then** it advances r per output and reaches K at the end; the own part is exactly ⌈K ÷ r⌉ device samples; `finished` arrives exactly on `playback_length_samples_in_mode` at every rate; the first 60 outputs are at full gain (within 0.1 dB of level 3's on a tone).
6. **Given** the click train, **When** played at each level in pitch-kept mode, **Then** the count of reproduced clicks per level is printed (reported).

### User Story 2 - A live control like the level (Priority: P2)

An app sets the mode before or during a playback, schedules it at a position, and reads every change in the log.

### Edge Cases

- A mode change at the playback's first output switches at once (no blend); a mode change at level 3 at once (no blend, the entry logged).
- A mode change and a level change at the same position: the mode blend runs and the level applies to the place at d(P); the new mechanism's frames read it.
- A mode request during a mode blend waits for the blend's end (the latest wins); a level request during a mode blend waits too; a mode request during a level crossfade in the default mode waits (the one wait rule).
- The recording's end during a blend: the old mechanism's remainder past the end is cut by the drain (spec 019's rule); a request past the latch is dropped and logged.
- A very short recording: the frames read zeros before 0 and past K as spec 013's resampler does; the own part is ⌈K ÷ r⌉ for every K ≥ 1 and the tail rule holds; asserted at K = 1, 59, 119 and 600 (own 2 / 99 / 199 / 1000 at level 1; tails 118 / 81 / 101 / 80).
- Level 3 in pitch-kept mode is the exact bypass (no frame grid runs); leaving level 3 anchors the grid at that output; returning to level 3 waits for a whole place and overlap-adds the current frame over its remaining overlap.
- The one-pass stream has no playback speed and no mode (the control is refused on the one-pass and record streams, as the level is).
- The processing record's timeline map (c) replays the nominal place through `place_at_in_mode` (the mode entries read; `place_at` keeps the default-mode reading); the audio heard in pitch-kept mode is within ±64 device samples (10.7 ms) of it (stated in the guide).
- Exact silence (the codec bypassed, stored zeros): every correlation is zero and the shift is 0 by the tie rule; the output is zero.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The engine MUST offer a playback speed mode with two values, the default (the pitch moving with the speed) and pitch kept, as a live control on the playback stream: a plain setter and getter kept by `reset` and `reconfigure`, and a scheduled change in the output clock; every change that moves the applied value MUST be a change entry, a scheduled same-value change a logged no-op, a plain same-value set not logged; an invalid value MUST be refused with a dedicated error (status 38); the control MUST be refused on the one-pass and record streams.

## Assumptions

- **Alternatives considered**: an emulator option as the kind (offering both mechanisms is something the device cannot do; both kinds land in the Workbench by rule, model internal names what the control selects); a boolean switch (a third mechanism would then need another control); a device control (the device's mechanism is unknown); a construction-time setting (a listener toggles it while listening); a decoder-domain method (the codec is a class match, so S-018's cycle-level method would be modeled on a decoder the device does not have); a spectral method (heavier, no better evidenced); Δ = 60 (the codec's full lag range, 1.5 × the search cost, the glide's p95 slightly worse); a crossfade for level changes in pitch-kept mode (the overlap-add is continuous; a blend would run two time-scalers for 20 ms); a fractional-shift refinement (30 dB at the band's top for about +57 multiply-adds per output); bounding the click doubling (reported until a target exists); a tolerance on the length (the house standard since 013 is exact); a fifth kind for the tier (a public enum for one control).
- **The device's mechanism is unknown** (A-054); this feature models neither reading of the evidence, it adds the behavior the research leans toward, in a class the research does not name, as an option, and leaves the default alone.
- **The design script's figures** (the spurious margin, the pitch readers' own error, the click counts, T_c, the shift sequences) are printed by a reference that shares only the published rules, and the harness pins each, as specs 013 and 019 did.
- **No device claim**: the option is an emulator convention; every description says so.
