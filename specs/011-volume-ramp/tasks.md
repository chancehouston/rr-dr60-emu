## Conventions that apply to every task

- **Traceability**: cite spec 011 FR, SC and A-045 (and A-044 for the law) in code comments, rustdoc and test names, e.g. `// A-045: a 20 ms knob movement (spec 011 FR-002)`, `// 011 FR-003: the range and running hold`, `// 011 FR-005: take the position at once`.
- **The six golden files do not move** (FR-006): `golden`, `golden_agc`, `golden_vas`, `golden_codec`, `golden_output`, `golden_output_clamp` and their SHA guards stay green through every task; every generator sets its route and volume before the first block, so a fresh engine takes the position at once (research R-04, R-06). Run all six after every phase.
- **The oracles** (research R-05): (a) the **splice identity** at `Tap::AfterVolumeAmp` in the stage-11-only configurations: bit-identical to the old-position run before the change, to the new-position run from transition sample N, the first differing sample 1 and the last differing sample N − 1; the instantaneous step is the splice itself (no hook); (b) the **gain sequence** in a core unit test (exact); (c) the **ratio reader** (4 f32 ULP, samples with |reference| ≥ 0.25 of the peak).
- **Indexing** (FR-001, FR-002): transition samples k = 1 … N, N = `rate * VOLUME_TRANSITION_MS / 1000` in integers (120, 160, 320, 882, 960, 1764, 1920); sample 1 is the first sample of the next block that processes at least one sample; sample N carries exactly 0.10's scalar.
- **The click energy** (FR-004; R-02, R-05): a Hann window of `round(0.5 · rate)` samples centred on the transition's midpoint (on the step for the reference), the energy at 20 Hz ≤ f ≤ 2750 Hz with |f − 1000 Hz| > 250 Hz; the 1 kHz cosine peaks on the change's output sample (placed through the path latency); earphone 0 dBFS, speaker −30 dBFS; ≥ 30 dB and within **±0.25 dB** of research R-02's table.
- **Floating point** (R-02, R-03): `u = k · inv_n`, `s = u·u·(3 − 2u)`, `knob = from + (to − from)·s`, `g = law(knob)` held by the range and running hold (upward `bound(g, g_prev, g_to)`, downward `bound(g, g_to, g_prev)`, 010's two-compare `bound`, never `f64::clamp`); plain `f64`, no platform math function.
- **Wording** (R-10): "the volume transition", "a volume change takes 20 ms"; "ramp" only in identifiers and the feature name; never "accurate"; no claim that the transition protects hearing or equipment.
- **Story boundaries (Principle VII)**: US1 uses the stage-11-only and output-only configurations and engines only (no registers, no C); US2 reuses the 006 – 010 equivalence, determinism, allocation, mutation and timing harnesses; US3 the cross-check and the audit.
- **Real-time safety**: the per-block calls allocate nothing; the transition's per-sample work is bounded (one divide, a few multiplies, two compares).
- **Measurement lessons that apply**: a 1 kHz tone at 6 kHz has six samples per period (sample peaks read phase, not level: use the oracles above, never a peak); the restated 009 checks need 8192-sample stimuli (with 4096 the slice from N is empty for late change points, research R-06); `cargo test --test a --test b NAME` filters every binary by NAME; timing figures only from a solo release run with `--test-threads=1`; a throwaway harness test needs the crate doc comment; `cargo llvm-cov` stops at the first failing test, so the new golden (red until blessed) is excluded from the gate before the bless.
- **Before ticking a task**: `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, `cargo clippy --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings` and `cargo test --all-features` must pass (inside a story's test-first window, that story's new tests are the expected red). The MSRV gate is `RUSTFLAGS="-D warnings" cargo +1.85 test --workspace --all-features --no-run`.
- **Commits**: Conventional Commits, on branch `011-volume-ramp`, after each task or logical group. Merge to `main` only through a green PR, and only after the user says so.
