# Research: Speed-Change Crossfade (019)

## R-01: The fade inside the stage

**Decision.** `SpeedStage` gains `fade: Option<Fade>`, `Fade { old_k: u32, old_p5: u64, n: u32 }`. `apply_request` runs only while `fade.is_none()` (and not latched): a request for a level other than the active one starts a fade (`old_k = active`, `old_p5 = p5`, `n = 0`, then `active = requested`); a request equal to the active level does nothing; the deferred switch into level 3 waits for a whole place as today (so a blend into 3 starts at a whole place). Per output while a fade runs: `n += 1`; `y_new` is the active level's value at `p5` as today; `y_old` is the old branch's constant-level value at `old_p5` (`x(i_old)` for `old_k == 5`, else `dot(bank(old_k), ph_old, i_old + D)`); for n < N `y = y_old + w[n − 1]·(y_new − y_old)`, and at n = N `y = y_new` itself (hardware B1, coach 1: `a + 1.0·(b − a)` is not `b` in floating point for about 44 % of pairs; the reference uses the identical rule); `old_p5 += step(old_k)`; when `n == N` the fade ends (`fade = None`), the next output being the new branch alone. The pulls follow the leading branch: `pull_through(max(base_old, base_new))` while a fade runs (the old branch leads when the old level is faster). The `dot` signature takes the bank slice; the ring and the pulls are shared. The "steady identity" fast path (level 3, no request pending, `fade.is_none()` tested explicitly, `pulled == i`) is unchanged, so a constant level 3 stays bit-identical (after a fade into level 3 from a faster level the general path runs for about 124 outputs while `pulled > i`, the same output by spec 013 FR-006, a cost noted); at the other levels a constant level never enters the fade branch, so FR-003 holds by construction (no fade → the 0.18 code path, sample for sample).

**Rationale.** D1, D4, D5; the hardware review's simulation (the same-place blend aliases; the time-domain crossfade reads 44.7 – 53.8 dB); the coach's finding that a 2550 Hz bank is not the identity at whole places (no level-3 branch is needed under D1).

**Alternatives.** The same-place blend (rejected: aliasing, the pitch step kept); restarting a fade on a new request (D4's alternative; the `fade_no_wait` mutant); a crossfade outside the stage at the host rate (rejected: the stage's place arithmetic is where both branches are exact).

## R-02: N and the weights

**Decision.** `speed::CROSSFADE_SAMPLES: u32 = 120` (20 ms of device samples, A-045's length restated as an engine convention) and `speed::CROSSFADE_MS: u32 = 20`, published in C as `RR_DR60_SPEED_CROSSFADE_MS 20` beside `RR_DR60_VOLUME_TRANSITION_MS` (product M1: the app debounces its stepper with it; pinned to the core constant; the only header change). The weights are a `[f64; 120]` table filled at construction with the pinned expression `u * u * (3.0 - 2.0 * u)`, `u = f64::from(n) / 120.0` (hardware B8: the expanded 3u² − 2u³ differs on 64 of 120 entries; the reference and the script use the same expression); the table costs nothing per sample and is bit-identical to computing per sample. Pins: `w[29] == 0.15625` exactly (u = 0.25: 0.0625·2.5 = 0.15625, exact in binary), `w[119] == 1.0` exactly, `w` strictly increasing, `w[0] > 0`. The design script prints the same table's two pinned values.

**Rationale.** D2, D3; SC-002.

**Alternatives.** Per-sample computation (equivalent; the table avoids a divide); equal-power weights (unneeded: the dip of an amplitude blend between incoherent branches is a few milliseconds).

## R-03: The ring and the pulls

**Decision.** `RING` 128 → 256. The two branches' places diverge by at most |k_old − k_new|·N ÷ 5 = 4·120 ÷ 5 = 96 stored samples over a fade (1 ↔ 5); the kernel is centred (i − 28 … i + 28, 57 taps), so the span is 96 + 57 = **153** either way round (hardware B4, coach 6): the highest index read is at most i_new + 124 (the old branch leading when the old level is faster), the lowest at least i_new − 124; 256 holds it with `pulled` at most i_new + 125. The pulls follow the leading branch (`pull_through(max(base_old, base_new))`); `pull_through` stays at most 2 + 29 per output. Debug assertions in `x()` and the fast dot path guard the ring (`j + RING > pulled`). The mask form (`& (RING − 1)`) needs the power of two.

**Rationale.** Hardware B4; FR-009.

**Alternatives.** 192 (not a power of two; the mask would become a modulo); keeping 128 and bounding the fade (would truncate 1 ↔ 5 fades).

## R-04: The latch, the tail and the end cut

**Decision.** The latch, `final_count`, the tail (`Tail` outputs) and the `Silence` boundary follow the new branch's `p5` exactly as in 0.18; a fade in progress continues through the tail (both branches read zeros past K through `x`) and is cut when the player stops requesting (the drain follows). The worst cut is the old branch's remaining fade samples at that moment: when the new level is faster the new branch reaches K first; the matrix reports per level pair, for a change at the latest landing that still starts a fade, the number of cut blend samples, the cut's peak amplitude (dBFS re the stimulus) and its energy, in `speed_only` and `speed_only_codec` (hardware B9, coach 10): with the codec on the recording ends in the stop's fed silence (≥ 60 device samples, spec 008), with it off the remainder is real content at weight 1 − w_n. **The length rule is unchanged for a constant level and for the new branch's trajectory** (hardware B3): a waited request lands up to 120 device samples later than 0.18's request did, so a schedule whose requests are under 20 ms apart plays longer than in 0.18 (the changes golden's `speed_inside` entries, two requests one device sample apart: their produced counts move, asserted as expected at bless time); the smoke's speed case (one change) keeps its count (asserted).

**Rationale.** D6, FR-006; the length rule stays spec 013's.

**Alternatives.** Extending the final count by the fade's remainder (rejected by D6: it changes `playback_length_samples` and the length tests for a mostly silent remainder).

## R-05: The mutants

**Decision.** `SpeedMutation` gains `fade_step: bool` (**no fade is created and the request applies at once**: 0.18 bit for bit, including requests inside 20 ms (hardware B2); it realizes the click reader's "instantaneous switch" reference and gates the re-bless: with it on, the committed `golden-speed-v1.json` and the changes golden's `speed_inside` entries regenerate bit for bit before they are re-blessed), `fade_linear: bool` (w = u), `fade_len_delta: i32` (N ± d, the table recomputed at construction) and `fade_no_wait: bool` (a request during a fade applies at once: the running fade's new branch becomes the old branch and the weight restarts at n = 1; D4's rejected alternative; hardware B7, coach 8). Each is caught by a named oracle: `fade_step` by the blend identity and the click table; `fade_linear` by the weight at ⌊N/4⌋ (0.25 vs 0.15625); `fade_len_delta` by the splice at s + N − 1 and the weight pins; `fade_no_wait` by the wait cases (the harness reference models the wait).

**Rationale.** D8, SC-002; the house pattern (every mutant has a named oracle that fails on it and passes on the default).

## R-06: The harness reference, the reader and the checks

**Decision.** `speed_checks::reference_output_at` gains the wait and the fade: it keeps `(old_k, old_p5, n)` beside the place, applies a request only when no fade runs, and blends with `value_at` for both branches and the published weights (read through a `__test_hooks::crossfade_weights()` hook or recomputed from the formula; the core pins both agree), narrowing after the blend. Spec 013's `us2_speed` schedules then stay bit-exact oracles with the reference updated once. The new reader `analysis::click_energy_in(y, rate, centre, window_s, exclusions_hz)`: a Hann window of `round(window_s·rate)` samples centred on `centre`, the power of the bins in 20 – 2750 Hz excluding each exclusion ± 250 Hz (an exclusion above the Nyquist frequency folds to rate − f; overlapping exclusions are a union); `click_energy` delegates with 0.5 s and `[1000.0]` (spec 011's figures unchanged, pinned by its tests). The 40 ms window at 6 kHz has 240 samples and 25 Hz bins; the step reader is centred on s (its click) and the fade reader on s + N ÷ 2; a held-level floor row is printed. *Restated at implementation (coach 1, hardware B1, B2):* the crossfade's reading sits 8 – 15 dB **above** the held-level floor on eighteen rows (its own residue, the two-pitch double image), so the ratio measures the blend, with 15.6 dB of margin at the worst row; on the 2.4 kHz 1 → 5 and 2 → 4 rows the output tones (1440 and 1920 Hz) sit off the 25 Hz grid and their leakage sets the floor, so there the assertion is a lower bound; a 50 ms reader (300 samples, 20 Hz bins, every output tone of the table on the grid) is reported beside it. All twenty rows are asserted ≥ 30 dB (the 1 kHz rows read 45.6 – 50.8 dB; coach 4); the voiced stimulus fills the band, so its two absolute energies are reported, never a ratio read as a failure. The stimulus is an 8000-sample tone with the request when the place reaches stored sample 4000 (hardware B3: the 0.5 s window must lie inside the own part; a window reaching the playback's onset read the onset's step). For the other six host rates the blend identity is checked on a device-rate trace (`__test_hooks::speed_device_outputs`, the speed stage's device outputs of the last play call), since the blend passes stage 10 and the resampler before the host output (coach 3); the partition identity is checked on the host output at every rate. `crossfade_checks.rs`: `blend_identity(rate, pair)` (the engine's playback through `speed_only` at 6 kHz and the default at every rate against the reference: 0 mismatches), `splice(rate, pair)` (bit-identical to the old constant run before s and to the new constant-level computation from s + N − 1), `click_table()` (the twenty rows of 013's step table on stored −6 dBFS tones at 6 kHz: the `fade_step` mutant's output as the switch, the 40 ms reader with both output tones excluded, the thirteen 2 and 2.4 kHz rows asserted ≥ 30 dB, the seven 1 kHz rows and the 0.5 s reader and the floor row reported), `voiced_click()` (reported), `wait_cases()` (consecutive requests, a superseded request, a request equal to the target, a request at the latch: the engine equals the reference bit for bit and the log holds every entry), `end_cut()` (count, peak and energy per pair in both configurations, reported), `partitions(rate)` (block sizes 1, 64, 4096, the irregular partitions through spec 015's schedule: identical outputs and logs), `lengths_unchanged(rate)` (013's `lengths` at every level, a fade in flight).

**Rationale.** D8; FR-007, FR-008, FR-011; hardware B1 (the 011 reader is unusable here), coach 3 and 5.

## R-07: The three re-pins

**Decision.** Before the re-bless, a gate: with `fade_step` on (0.18's behavior) the speed golden's generator and the changes golden's `speed_inside` scenarios regenerate the committed files bit for bit (hardware B2). At bless time `generate_speed(bless)` asserts that every entry without a schedule is byte-identical to the committed file (the constant-level entries), that every scheduled entry's changes land before the end **and its blend completes before K** (coach 12); `generate_changes(bless)` asserts that every entry but the `speed_inside` scenarios is byte-identical and that the `speed_inside` produced counts moved as the wait predicts (hardware B3); `smoke_reference::SMOKE_SPEED_HASH` is re-pinned from the Rust path and `SMOKE_SPEED_SAMPLES` asserted unchanged (one change, no wait), `smoke.c`'s defines following. The eleven other audio golden guards are unmoved (their configurations have no speed schedule), as are every other smoke hash.

**Rationale.** D7, FR-003; coach 2.

## R-08: Cost

**Decision.** `tests/crossfade_timing.rs` against 0.18's public API (the default front, the codec bypassed, a recorded 30 s stimulus played at a constant level 3 and at a constant level 5: `level3_ns=`, `level5_ns=`), copied into the 13defc7 worktree by a `crossfade` mode of `scripts/ab-timing.sh` that asserts both within 3 % (`RR_DR60_CROSSFADE_COST_CEILING` 0.05 on the label job; a step in `.github/workflows/ab-timing.yml`); level 5 exercises the general path where the fade test and the re-signatured `dot` live (coach 4). `crossfade_fades_timing.rs` head-only: the same stimulus with a level change every 200 ms cycling 3 → 5 → 1 → 3 (every fade 120 device samples of two dots), the per-sample cost reported and the worst 4096-sample block **asserted** against the real-time budget (1000 × 4096 ÷ 48 = 85 333 µs). Expected: the constant-level cost moves by one `fade.is_none()` test on both paths (under 1 %); a fade's extra work is 57 multiply-adds per device sample for 120 samples.

**Rationale.** D9, FR-009.

**Figures (2026-10-30, a solo run of `scripts/ab-timing.sh 13defc7 crossfade`, this machine, 45 runs × 3 alternations).** Level 3: 13defc7 26.23 ns, the working tree 26.34 ns per host sample, **+0.10 ns (+0.40 %)**; level 5: 28.87 → 28.91 ns, **+0.04 ns (+0.15 %)**; both PASS at the 3 % budget. A change every 200 ms (3 → 5 → 1 → 3): 28.48 ns per host sample, +2.14 ns (+8.13 %) over level 3; the worst 4096-sample block 218.2 µs of the 85 333 µs budget. On the CI runner (x86-64, PR #49's `ab-timing` label, run 41165761605): level 3 61.89 → 62.44 ns, **+0.56 ns (+0.90 %)**; level 5 65.80 → 66.86 ns, **+1.06 ns (+1.61 %)**; both PASS at the 5 % ceiling; a change every 200 ms 65.55 ns, +3.10 ns (+4.97 %) over level 3, the worst block 361.5 µs of 85 333.

## R-09: The design script and the records

**Decision.** `speed_design.py --crossfade` prints the weight table's pinned values (w₃₀ = 0.15625, w₁₂₀ = 1.0, from the pinned expression), the twenty rows' click reductions under the 40 ms and the 0.5 s readers with the floor (the simulation of the hardware review, now the script's own: the harness pins each within 0.5 dB), the worst end cut per pair and the ring span (153). The records follow records.md: A-054 amended **as a hypothesis** (product M2: "The emulator's crossfade is a convention, not a model of the device. What the device does at the moment of a level change is unknown: no document describes it. Because the decoder works in 20 ms frames, a switch at a frame boundary is one candidate; an instant step and a brief mute remain candidates. Capture P1 decides."), with the phrases "engine convention" and "capture P1" pinned by the registers cross-check (coach 11); capture P1 reworded (a sustained 2.3 kHz tone and a vowel with the dial turned, 48 kHz, read for a step, a fade or a gap and for 20 ms alignment) in A-054 and the issue template; stage 9's **description** carries the blend ("…at one of five playback speeds (level 3 is normal); a level change blends over 20 ms, an engine convention") and its coverage note is rewritten whole within 120 characters keeping the pinned tokens ("the device's own decoder (frame length, look-ahead, codebooks); its speed method and the sound of a level change (A-054)"), the controls note unchanged (coach 2, product M4); the manifest canonical text, README table and guide blocks re-blessed once; the CHANGELOG under Changed: "an audio change at level changes only, not an assumption change: every constant level is bit-identical to 0.18; A-054 amended in wording, the device's behavior at a change still open", naming the two constants and the C define (product M6); the guide's playback-speed paragraph says the instant switch's step is replaced by a 20 ms blend and quotes the harness's click-energy reduction per pair (never "no longer ticks"; product M3, hardware B10), with the onset sentence and the end cut; the product notes of records.md plus investigation-mode.md's § 5 bullet, brief.md's engine-facts clause and monetization.md's stale parenthetical (product M5); CLAUDE.md's status paragraph.

**Rationale.** FR-002, FR-010; product M3, M4; hardware B8.
