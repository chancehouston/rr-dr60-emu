# Feature Specification: Speed-Change Crossfade

**Feature Branch**: `019-speed-crossfade`

**Created**: 2026-10-30

**Status**: Draft reviewed (coach 1 – 14, hardware B1 – B8, product M1 – M5 applied 2026-10-30); owner sign-off pending

**Input**: User description: "019 speed-change crossfade (spec 013's follow-up B2; the owner's engine order 018 → 019, decisions.md 2026-10-29; controls-discussion.md § 4 item 6 "removes the tick on a level change to or from levels 4 – 5; a constant level stays bit-identical", § 2b row "Speed crossfade"). As a listener and as an app developer, I want a playback speed-level change to blend from the old level into the new over a short fixed time instead of switching in one sample, so that a change to or from the fast levels no longer ticks on voice content above about 1.6 kHz, while a constant level stays bit-identical to 0.18. […] Out of scope: pitch-kept speed (c1), the live playback-stage bypass crossfade (spec 021), any change to the ratios or the banks' bands, record-side behavior."

## Decisions for sign-off

The owner reads this section first. Each decision gives the recommendation; the alternative is in the Assumptions section. The three draft reviews (coach 1 – 14, hardware B1 – B8, product M1 – M5) are applied at their defaults below; the draft's same-place blend (the first D1 and D5) was reversed by the hardware review's simulation and the coach's identity finding, and the points the reviewers raised as owner questions are D1, D7 (the three re-pins), D8's criterion and D6's cut.

| # | Decision | Recommended |

| D1 | **A level change is a time-domain crossfade, not a switch.** From the device sample a change lands on (today's landing: the plain setter's first computed device sample, or spec 015's `speed_landing(P, rate)`; a change into level 3 first waits for a whole place as today), **two branches run for N device samples**: the **old branch** keeps its own place, ratio and band and fades out; the **new branch** starts at the common place at the new ratio and band and fades in; the output is y_old + w_n·(y_new − y_old), the two branches being spec 013's constant-level computations at their own places. ## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-30 (the draft reviews: coach 1 – 14, hardware B1 – B8, product M1 – M5)

- **The same-place blend reversed** (hardware B2, B3; coach 1): the old band at the new ratio aliases above the device Nyquist for changes into the fast levels, and both branches keep the pitch step; the time-domain crossfade (D1) removes the tick with no aliasing (the hardware review's simulation, `xfade_sim.py` on the design script's own banks, reproduced by this session: 44.7 – 53.8 dB with the 40 ms reader, 19.8 – 48.1 dB with the 0.5 s reader, on the twelve pairs; the harness reproduces the figures before any record quotes them).
- **No level-3 branch** (hardware B5; coach 1): a 2550 Hz interpolation bank's phase 0 is not the identity (centre tap 0.94, 0.058 off a unit impulse); under D1 none is needed. If a coherent variant is ever wanted, a Nyquist(5) prototype at a 3000 Hz cutoff has phase 0 = δ to 1e-16.
- **The click reader** (hardware B1; coach 3): spec 011's reader is unusable here (the exclusion is hard-coded at 1 kHz; a −6 dBFS tone inside 20 – 2750 Hz dominates both windows: simulated ratios 0.4, 0.1, −4.3 dB); the new reader of D8(c) excludes both output tones and uses a 40 ms window; the "instantaneous switch" is the harness reference with a step weight, since the engine no longer switches.
- **Three re-pins, not one** (coach 2): the changes golden's speed scenarios and the C smoke's speed case move with the speed golden's schedule entries; every constant-level entry and every other guard are unmoved (D7).
- **The wait's semantics** (hardware B7; coach 4, 5, 10): readings and entries carry target levels, chained; a superseded request is logged and never heard; a waited request equal to the running target starts nothing; a waited request at the latch is dropped; "the next playback starts at the latest request"; the harness reference models the wait, so US2 AS1 is bit-exact rather than "monotonic".
- **The ring** (hardware B4): the two branches' places diverge by up to 96 stored samples (1 ↔ 5), plus the 57 taps and the 28-sample look-ahead: 153 > the stage's 128-sample ring; the plan sizes it (256, a power of two for the mask).
- **The end-of-recording cut** (hardware B6): stated in D6; the worst cut reported, the length rule unchanged.
- **The records** (product M3, M4; hardware B8): the README has no tick sentence (the guide's playback-speed section has it); A-054's amendment says the crossfade is the emulator's convention, that the device's natural quantum is the 20 ms CELP frame so a frame-boundary switch is the plausible device behavior, and P1 asks for a sustained 2.3 kHz tone and a vowel with the dial turned, read for a step, a fade or a gap and for 20 ms alignment; the stage 9 note says "a 20 ms blend at a level change (engine convention)"; the CHANGELOG says "not a device behavior".
- **The app** (product M2, M5): a stepper, one change per gesture (the device has a button); nothing new from the engine (the onset is a constant plus the documented latency; the log gives the position); the blend's length may be published as a constant beside `VOLUME_TRANSITION_MS`.
- **Blend indexing** (coach 8): blend samples n = 1 … N, sample 1 at the landing s; the constant-level identity from device sample s + N − 1.
- **Edge cases added** (coach 9, 10, 12): a change scheduled at position 0 before `start_playback` is a start value (no blend, no entry); consecutive-position requests (the first starts a blend, the last of the rest waits); the one-pass never carries the speed.
- The step figures quoted are spec 013 B2's, pinned by `speed_checks::change_step` from `speed_design.py` (−77 … −99 dB at 1 kHz; −0.7 … −1.9 dB re the tone at 2 kHz to or from level 5; about 0 dB at 2.4 kHz; −40 dB between levels 2 and 4).

## Overview

Spec 013 made the playback speed a live control with five levels and chose, for the moment of a level change, to switch in one sample: the output after a change at a place is simply the new level's value there, which keeps the place exact and the oracle simple. The cost, quantified then and documented in the README and the guide, is a tick on content above about 1.6 kHz when a change involves the fast levels, because the fast levels' band is narrower than the slow levels' and the missing content is gated on or off at once. This feature replaces the switch by a short crossfade: the old level fades out at its own place and pitch while the new level fades in from the change, so no band and no content is gated within one sample. A constant level is untouched; only the 20 ms around a change sounds different.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Change the speed without a tick (Priority: P1)

A listener changes the playback speed while a recording plays, by the control or by a scheduled change, and hears the speed and pitch move without a click, including changes to or from the fast levels on voice.

**Why this priority**: it is the whole feature; everything else describes its edges.

**Independent Test**: a recorded −6 dBFS tone at 2 kHz played with a change from level 3 to level 5 at a known position: the output before the change equals the level-3 run, from device sample s + N − 1 on equals the level-5 constant-level computation from the common place, each blend sample equals y_old + w·(y_new − y_old) of the two branches at their own places to the bit, and the click energy around the change (the 40 ms reader of D8) is at least 30 dB below the instantaneous switch's.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a recording playing at a constant level, **When** no change is made, **Then** every output sample is bit-identical to 0.18's at that level (the own part, the tail and the drain), at all seven host rates.
2. **Given** a change from level a to level b landing at device sample s (blend samples n = 1 … N, sample 1 at s), **When** the N blend samples are read, **Then** each equals y_a + w_n·(y_b − y_a), y_a level a's constant-level value at the old branch's own place (advancing at level a's ratio from s) and y_b level b's at the common place (advancing at level b's ratio), to the bit, with w(⌊N/4⌋) = 0.15625 exactly and w_N = 1; the output before s equals level a's run; from s + N − 1 on it equals level b's constant-level computation from the common place.
3. **Given** stored −6 dBFS tones at 1, 2 and 2.4 kHz and the level pairs of spec 013's step table at 6 kHz in the speed-only configuration, **When** each change is made by the engine and by the harness reference with a step weight, **Then** the crossfade's click energy (the 40 ms reader excluding both output tones) is at least 30 dB below the switch's; the 0.5 s reader's figure and the voiced stimulus's reduction are reported.
4. **Given** a change scheduled at position P on the playback stream, **When** the playback is run at block sizes 1, 64 and 4096 and the irregular partitions, **Then** the outputs and the logs are identical, and the change entry sits at P.
5. **Given** a change into level 3 at a non-whole place, **When** it lands, **Then** the blend starts at the next whole place (at most four device samples later, spec 013's rule) and the constant level 3 after the blend is exactly the stored samples.
6. **Given** a change whose blend would cross the end of the recording's own part, **When** it is made, **Then** the new branch's own part and tail end by spec 013's rule for the new level, the old branch's remainder is cut by the unchanged drain (the worst cut reported), and both branches read zeros past K.

### User Story 2 - The change is bounded and described (Priority: P2)

An app developer relies on the blend's timing and cost being fixed and documented: a change's audible onset, what a second change during a blend does, what the engine says about itself, and that nothing else moved.

**Independent Test**: a second change requested during a blend lands when the blend completes, at most 20 ms later, and is logged at its own position; the manifest's stage 9 names the crossfade; the cost A/B at a constant level is within budget.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a change requested (or scheduled) while a blend is running, **When** the blend completes, **Then** the new level's blend begins at that sample (after the deferred-switch wait for level 3), the superseded intermediate request (if any) never sounds, every request is logged as a change entry at its position with chained target levels, `playback_speed()` reads the latest target, and the output equals the harness reference, which models the wait, bit for bit (one blend at a time, each with weights 0 < w_1 < … < w_N = 1).
2. **Given** the control manifest and the stage manifest, **When** read, **Then** the control manifest is unchanged (no new entry), and stage 9's notes name "a 20 ms blend at a level change (engine convention)" at model version 0.19.0, its coverage note names the change's own sound as not modeled, and A-054 is amended.
3. **Given** the cost A/B against 0.18 at a constant level, **When** run solo, **Then** the per-sample cost is within 3 % (5 % on the runner), and the blend's extra work for 20 ms and the worst block are reported within the real-time budget.
4. **Given** the C API and the Swift package, **When** built and tested, **Then** the header is byte-identical, the parity tests pass (the C path blends exactly as the Rust path), and the C smoke's speed case is re-pinned once.

### Edge Cases

- A change to the level already active: no blend, no change in output; the entry is logged as today (before == after).
- A change requested in the same call as the playback's start, or scheduled at position 0 before `start_playback` (`speed_landing(0) = 0`; the schedule survives the start): the start value rule of spec 015 (no blend, no entry).
- Consecutive-position requests (several levels one sample apart): the first starts a blend, the last of the rest waits and lands at the blend's end; each is logged.
- The one-pass never carries the speed (spec 015 D1): bit-identical to 0.18 in every one-pass configuration.
- A change at the last device sample of the own part, or during the tail: the new branch's length rule governs, both branches read zeros past K, the old branch's remainder is cut by the drain; nothing is added past the drain.
- Two changes scheduled at the same position: the later wins, one blend (spec 015's rule).
- A playback stopped during a blend: the next playback starts at the latest requested level (the waited one, if any) with no blend (a stream's first sample).
- At 6 kHz the blend is 120 device samples = 120 host samples; at other host rates the blend's length in host samples is N·rate ÷ 6000 through the reconstruction and conversion, and the onset is reported like spec 015's speed onset.
- A change into level 3 requested at a non-whole place while a blend is running: the blend completes, then the deferred-switch wait, then the blend into 3.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: A level change MUST produce, from the device sample s it lands on, N blend samples n = 1 … N that are y_old + w_n·(y_new − y_old): the old level's constant-level value at the old branch's own place (advancing at the old ratio from s) and the new level's at the common place (advancing at the new ratio), w the published smoothstep (w_N exactly 1); before s the output MUST equal the old level's run and from device sample s + N − 1 the new level's constant-level computation from the common place, bit for bit.
- **FR-002**: N MUST be 20 ms of device samples (120), a documented engine convention (A-054 amended: the device's behavior at a change is open, its natural quantum the 20 ms frame; capture P1 reworded).
- **FR-003**: At a constant level every output MUST be bit-identical to 0.18.0 in every configuration: the own part, the tail and the drain, at every level including level 3's stored-sample identity, at all seven host rates; the one-pass in every configuration; every constant-level golden entry, the eleven audio golden guards without a speed schedule and every other smoke hash unmoved; the speed golden's schedule entries, the changes golden's speed scenarios and the C smoke's speed case re-pinned once each.
- **FR-004**: Level 3 MUST need no interpolation: a blend out of level 3 reads the old branch at whole places, a blend into level 3 starts at the deferred whole place (spec 013's rule kept), and a constant level 3 outside a blend stays exactly the stored samples.
- **FR-005**: A request during a blend MUST wait for the blend's end (then the deferred-switch wait for level 3) and start its own blend; the latest request wins; a superseded request is logged and never sounds; a waited request equal to the running target starts nothing; a waited request reaching the latch is dropped, its entry logged; entries and `playback_speed()` carry target levels, chained; the audible onset of a waited request is documented.
- **FR-006**: The blend MUST NOT change the recording's length rule, the latch, the tail or the drain: `playback_length_samples` and spec 013's length tests hold on the new branch's place; both branches read zeros past K; the old branch's remainder past the new branch's end is cut by the drain and the worst cut is reported.
- **FR-007**: The click energy of a change MUST be at least 30 dB below the instantaneous switch's under this spec's reader (a 40 ms Hann window centred on the blend's midpoint, 20 – 2750 Hz, the old and the new output tones ± 250 Hz excluded, at 6 kHz in the speed-only configuration; the switch realized by the harness reference with a step weight), on stored −6 dBFS tones at 1, 2 and 2.4 kHz for the level pairs of spec 013's step table; the 0.5 s reader's figure and the voiced stimulus's reduction reported.
- **FR-008**: Scheduled changes MUST be partition-independent at every host rate (the outputs and the logs identical at block sizes 1, 64, 4096 and the irregular partitions); the setter oracle of spec 015 MUST hold.
- **FR-009**: Deterministic and real-time safe as every spec: no new bank, no allocation in a call, the ring sized at construction for the two branches' span; the per-sample cost at a constant level within 3 % of 0.18 (5 % on the runner); the blend's cost and the worst block reported within the real-time budget.
- **FR-010**: The C API and the control manifest MUST be unchanged; stage 9's manifest notes MUST name "a 20 ms blend at a level change (engine convention)" at model version 0.19.0 and its coverage note the change's own sound; A-054 MUST be amended (the convention, the frame quantum, the P1 question); the CHANGELOG MUST record the convention change (visible in schedule outputs, not a device behavior; not an assumption change); the guide's playback-speed section and the product notes (controls-discussion § 2b and § 4 item 6, decisions.md) MUST be updated; the README needs no change; version 0.19.0.
- **FR-011**: Spec 013's harness reference MUST apply the same crossfade and the wait rule from its own published coefficients, so that 013's schedule tests stay bit-exact oracles; the weight sequence 0 < w_1 < … < w_N = 1 MUST be pinned by a core test.

### Out of Scope

- Pitch-kept speed (mechanism c1): a later option, decided by capture P1 or an app request.
- The live playback-stage bypass with a crossfade (spec 021).
- Any change to the five ratios, the banks' bands, the look-ahead or the length rule.
- Record-side behavior: the speed control is playback only.

### Key Entities

- **Blend**: the N device samples after a landing, each y_old + w·(y_new − y_old) of the two branches; its weights are published and fixed.
- **The two branches**: the old level's constant-level computation at its own place (fading out) and the new level's at the common place (fading in), each spec 013's computation unchanged.
- **Waited request**: a level requested during a blend, applied at the blend's end.

## Assumptions

- **Alternatives considered**: the same-place coherent blend (the draft's D1; rejected by the hardware review's simulation: aliasing above the device Nyquist for changes into the fast levels, the pitch step kept, 10 dB worse than the switch on a 2.4 kHz tone); a longer or shorter blend (D2: 20 ms is the engine's one transition length); a linear weight or an equal-power weight (D3: the dip of an amplitude blend between incoherent branches is a few milliseconds and inaudible; the linear weight is a mutant); completing a running blend at once on a new request (D4: a small step; rejected for smoothness); a level-3 interpolation branch (D5: unnecessary; a 2550 Hz bank is not the identity at whole places); snapping level 3 to a non-whole place (rejected by spec 013 D5); extending the length rule for the old branch's remainder (D6: rejected; mostly silence); an emulator option off by default (D7: the default sound would keep the tick, and an A/B between two emulator conventions has no Workbench value).
- **The convention is not a device claim**: A-054 is amended to say the crossfade is the emulator's convention for the moment of a level change; the device's natural change quantum is the 20 ms CELP frame, so a frame-boundary switch without a fade is the plausible device behavior; capture P1 (a sustained 2.3 kHz tone and a vowel with the dial turned, 48 kHz, read for a step, a fade or a gap and for 20 ms alignment) decides.
- **The branches read the same stored samples**: no extra look-ahead beyond the banks' 28 samples; the ring holds the two branches' divergence (up to 96 stored samples) plus the taps and the look-ahead.
- **The app** uses a stepper (one change per gesture, as the device's button); no new engine read-out is needed (product M2, M5).
