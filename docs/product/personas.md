The app describes only what the recorder's signal chain does to sound. Personas are described by what they do, not by what they believe.

## Jobs, ranked by how many users notice

1. Record, stop, play back, share. The device's own loop; the speaker is muted during recording (service manual, S-002), so "what was recorded" and "what you hear" are separate objects.
2. Hear the pauses vanish (VAS) and the onsets clip. The defining behaviour users describe.
3. Hear the speaker and the earphone differ.
4. See what is modeled and what is checked against a real unit.
5. Take the chain apart (taps, bypasses). A small, loud minority.

## Cheapest ways to learn more

- Mine the reviews of GhostTube EVP, Necrophonic, Spirit Box EMF Ghost Detector and Ghost Recorder (about 2,000 texts) for praise and complaint themes, mentions of the DR60, "real/fake", sound, subscriptions, ads, export.
- Five to eight interviews with people who own, owned or want an RR-DR60 (comments on the Higgypop articles, the YouTube videos featuring the device, DR60/EVP groups). Every owner is also a capture candidate; see the "Hardware evidence" issue template.
