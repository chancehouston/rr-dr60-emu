# Product decisions

A log of product decisions with the alternatives considered, the evidence, and the cost of reversing. Add a row when a decision is made or changed. Engineering decisions live in the specs.

| Date | Decision | Alternatives | Evidence | Reversal cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-11 | Positioning: "reproduces the RR-DR60's signal chain stage by stage, from the service manual, and shows which stages are checked against a real unit" | "DR60 emulator" (taken by GhostTube EVP); generic EVP recorder | competitors.md; the sources and assumptions registers | low; copy only |
| 2026-10-11 | Free tier is the full device with the device's limits (60 min, 99 recordings); paid tier is only what the device cannot do | gate part of the chain; unlimited free; subscription | monetization.md; category review themes | raising a cap is cheap, lowering one is not |
| 2026-10-11 | One non-consumable purchase; no subscription, ads or consumables at launch | subscription; ads | App Store Review Guideline 3.1.2(a); competitor reviews | one-time to subscription for new content is possible; the reverse is not |
| 2026-10-11 | App name contains no trademark ("Panasonic", "RR-DR60", "DR60") and nothing confusingly similar; the device is named once in the description with a trademark notice | name with "DR60" | App Store Review Guidelines 2.3.7, 5.2.1; Google Play "Misleading claims" | renaming after launch loses ratings and search position |
| 2026-10-11 | No analytics or advertising SDK at launch; App Privacy and Data safety both "data not collected" | third-party analytics | Apple App Privacy details; Play Data safety (on-device processing is not collection) | low |
| 2026-10-11 | Live monitoring during recording is not offered as a device behaviour (the speaker amp is muted while recording, S-002); if offered later it is a labelled Workbench extra | live monitor in the free tier | S-002; Android latency variance | low |
| 2026-10-11 | The evidence program (community test recordings, a loaned unit, purchase as a last resort) is a product priority | engineering-only | without a stage checked against a real unit the pitch is weaker than competitors' unsupported claims | n/a |
| 2026-10-11 | The engine is distributed as a Swift Package wrapping an XCFramework (and later an Android archive), not as a source port | source port | adoption speed is the library's value; a port forks the model | low now, high after the app exists |
| 2026-10-11 | The engine's next product-driven feature is a split between recording into an encoded, device-sized recording and playing it back through the speaker or earphone path | keep the single mic-to-speaker pass | the app's core loop is record, stop, play, share; the device mutes the speaker while recording | grows with every stage added before the split |
| 2026-10-11 | The vintage-gear audience stays in the copy but is not built for until evidence of it appears | build a dedicated mode | no evidence found | none |
| 2026-10-11 | Year-one goal is credibility: 1.0 ships fully free with the device's limits; the Workbench purchase follows in 1.1 after review mining | revenue from launch | monetization.md; the category's one-star themes are all about trust | low; adding a purchase later is routine |
| 2026-10-11 | Evidence program runs the free routes first (a published capture kit, requests to creators who own units); a purchase is considered only if those fail within about eight weeks | buy a unit now; never buy | second-hand asking prices EUR 1,700–3,500 (competitors.md) | none |
| 2026-10-11 | The inside view ships reduced in 1.0 (built stages live, labelled empty sockets for unbuilt stages, status badges and header, no touch controls) and replaces the separate "About this model" screen, with a flat text fallback for assistive technology and review notes | ship the inside with the Workbench in 1.1; keep a separate About screen | front-and-inside.md; the year-one credibility goal | moderate; the flat list is the fallback either way |
| 2026-10-11 | The Workbench keeps each recording's raw microphone capture for "Listen here", visible in the recording's details with a delete option and listed on the privacy label; the free tier keeps only the device-rate render | no raw capture (no "Listen here"); raw capture in the free tier | front-and-inside.md › Tap to inspect | low; deleting stored captures is a migration step |
| 2026-10-11 | The inside is rendered procedurally from one style sheet; commissioned illustration is revisited after the first reviews | commissioned illustration; a literal schematic | front-and-inside.md › Scope | low |
| 2026-10-11 | The inside is the second App Store screenshot at launch, with the status header visible | front-only screenshots | front-and-inside.md › Status badges; competitors.md (no competitor shows what it modeled) | low |
| 2026-10-11 | The engine may export short, neutral, English UI-facing strings per stage (the stage manifest, E-D); apps localize on their side | strings only in the app; a separate manifest file | front-and-inside.md › Engine requests | low; strings can move to a resource later |
| 2026-10-11 | No global controls on the inside; every Workbench control lives in the tapped component's card, a dot marks a component off the device default, and each card has one "device default" button | a settings page; a global reset | front-and-inside.md › Rules | low |
| 2026-10-11 | Public wording is "The front is the recorder. The inside is the model."; "experiment" stays internal | "The front is the product. The inside is the experiment." | front-and-inside.md; in this category "experiment" reads as a claim | none; copy only |
| 2026-10-12 | Product copy says "checked against a real unit", never "verified" (the stage manifest's status vocabulary; "verified" in the assumption register means sourced from a document, a different sense) | "verified" | spec 007 manifest status words; front-and-inside.md › Status badges | none; copy only |
