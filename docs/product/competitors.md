## What the table says

- The RR-DR60's name already sells apps: one emulation claim and three "DR60 sound bank" claims. None publishes what was modeled or how.
- The category accepts cheap annual subscriptions when the free mode works (GhostTube), accepts $9.99 one-time (Necrophonic), and punishes weekly plans, paywalls before a trial, and ads.
- Every one-star theme is about trust: "fake", "random", "same every time", "paid and it still didn't work".
- Claims this project does not make, as competitors make them: "spirit communication", "responses from ghosts", "detects humanoid bodies", and "closely matches" without published evidence.

## The gap this project occupies

A per-stage model of the whole signal chain, built from the service manual and datasheets, with a public sources and assumptions register, an open-source engine, and a measurement harness. The app can show, stage by stage, what is documented, what is assumed and what is verified. No competitor does this.
