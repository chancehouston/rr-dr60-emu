# Product notes

Notes kept by the `product-specialist` consulting agent (see `.claude/agents/product-specialist.md`) about the app built on this engine and about the engine as a product. They are working notes, not commitments; dates on each decision say when it was made. Engineering decisions live in the specs.

- **[brief.md](brief.md): the current product brief** (2026-10-16). Start here. It reconciles the design dossier of 2026-10-15 with the earlier notes, separates agreed direction from open questions, and lists the engine's capabilities at 0.11.0 against the dossier's questions. Where an older note disagrees, the brief wins; superseded passages are marked in place.
- [design-dossier-2026-10-15.pdf](design-dossier-2026-10-15.pdf): the design dossier *One instrument. Two ways to explore.* (v0.1, 2026-10-15), the source of the brief's agreed direction, with the three concept images. Its renderings are illustrative and its sensor readings are invented for the concept.
- [personas.md](personas.md): who the app and the library serve, and the job each user hires them for.
- [competitors.md](competitors.md): apps in the same category on the App Store and Google Play, with the claims they make.
- [monetization.md](monetization.md): the free tier, the paid tier, and the models considered.
- [front-and-inside.md](front-and-inside.md): the app's two views of the recorder (the front and the inside), what the inside may show, the free/Workbench split, and the engine requests it needs.
- [decisions.md](decisions.md): the product decision log.

Everything here follows the project's claims policy in `CLAUDE.md`: describe what the signal chain does to sound, say "modeled on" until a stage is checked against a real unit, and make no claim about paranormal phenomena either way.
