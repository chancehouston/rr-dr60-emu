One back panel.
A working signal path.

The familiar recorder silhouette stays in the bezel and transport. Flip to a procedural exposed interior: green board, copper traces, modular stage blocks, empty sockets, and the selected component opened in a lower work surface.

Click a stage on the board to inspect it. Adjust the limiter model values, change microphone sensitivity, or switch to playback to work with the output stage. “Device default” restores the selected component. These controls are visual mock interactions; they do not process audio.

WATCH THE DEVICE

Input level, limiter gain reduction, VAS state, memory, and the muted speaker are shown at the stage where they belong.

WORK THE COMPONENT

Each selected part owns its controls. A modified value appears on the board; global settings stay off the schematic.

KEEP THE CHAIN HONEST

The two unmodeled parts remain empty sockets. Record and playback light different paths; playback controls do not affect recording.

ENGINEER’S FLOW

Inspect → adjust → compare with device default → listen to the resulting chain. Component changes have context and a visible return path.
