- **Speech codec** ([spec 008](specs/008-celp-codec/spec.md), 2026-10-14; version **0.8.0**). Signal-chain stages 7 and 9 are modeled: a 4 kbit/s CELP-class coder of the period, an algebraic CELP of the G.729 / G.723.1 family adapted to the device's 6 kHz clock (20 ms frames of 80 bits, a 10 ms look-ahead, 180 device samples of delay; an 8th-order predictor sent as line spectral pairs, a fractional-lag adaptive codebook, a 17-bit four-pulse fixed codebook per 10 ms subframe, two gains per subframe with an energy predictor, a perceptual weighting filter in the search and a postfilter on playback), in the record path, the playback path and the one-pass processing. **Modeled on the class, not on the device's own coder, and not measured against a unit** (A-004 adopted as a class match; A-040 the frame length and look-ahead, A-041 the structure, bit allocation and post-processing, both low confidence; S-008 – S-011 registered as secondary sources for the class). The memory stores the frames; the stop completes the last frame by feeding silence and reports how much; record-then-play stays bit-identical to the one-pass processing. The coder is fixed-point integer arithmetic throughout, so its frames and output are bit-identical on every target and in the debug and release profiles. The model's figures against the ITU-T G.723.1 reference coder of the same bit density (`specs/008-celp-codec/calibration.md`): output level within 0.4 dB, third-octave levels 315 – 2000 Hz within 2.5 dB, log-spectral distance 1.8 – 2.3 dB, segmental SNR 9.8 – 12.0 dB, digital silence at −78 dBFS and below, delay 180 ± 2 device samples at the seven host rates. The fixed-gain predictor's mean was set to 54 dB (the design tool's first figure, 60, left 2.5 dB of margin at the quiet end of the level anchor A-029 behind an arithmetic error) at the post-implementation hardware review, before the codec golden was blessed.

- **Record and playback paths with a modeled memory** ([spec 006](specs/006-record-playback-split/spec.md), 2026-10-12; version **0.6.0**). The engine now has the device's structure: a **record path** (the limiter, the record filter and clamp, the VAS) writes into a **memory** of 60 minutes and 99 recordings (S-001; the capacity is a setting, `MemorySettings` / `memory_capacity_s` and `memory_max_recordings`, 1 s – 24 h and 1 – 9 999, above the device's figures "not the device's"), and a **playback path** (the playback filter) plays a recording back toward a speaker or earphone **route**. Recordings are numbered in the order made and renumbered on erase; recording stops by itself at the exact device sample at which the memory fills and keeps the partial recording (A-039); a start is refused when no time or no slot remains; paused time costs nothing in drop mode and is stored as silence in mute mode. Each recording and each playback starts from the path's reset state (A-037). Record-then-play is **bit-identical to the one-pass processing** (196 of 217 golden entries, the remaining 21 being the host-rate "after AGC" tap); the stop **flushes** the record path's latency so every fed sample is recorded, and the playback **drains** to settled silence after the stored samples (121 device samples of stage 10's response plus the rate conversion's tail; reported by `drain_samples`). The stored form is the record path's 32-bit device-rate output in this version (it becomes encoded frames when the codec is modeled, A-004); a recording leaves and enters the memory as an opaque, versioned byte sequence (magic `RRDR60RC`, form version 1, a trailing CRC-32; [contract](specs/006-record-playback-split/contracts/recording-form.md)) that any engine of this version imports at any host rate with bit-identical playback, and that is refused with a reason when truncated, altered, foreign or too large for the remaining memory.

- **Record-path automatic gain control** ([spec 002](specs/002-agc/spec.md)), signal-chain stage 3, **on by default**. It raises quiet input and lowers loud input toward a target level with a 10:1 slope, which produces level "pumping" after loud sounds and background noise that rises during pauses.   - the record stage (signal-chain stage 4) is the transmit band-pass: loss within −0.15 … +0.2 dB from 225 to 2250 Hz, 0.31 dB at 2550 Hz (datasheet typical 0.4), at least 20 dB attenuation at 45 Hz and zeros at DC;
  - the playback stage (stage 10) is the receive low-pass only, which **passes DC** (the 0.3 stage rejected it): loss within −0.15 … +0.25 dB from DC to 2250 Hz, 0.48 dB at 2550 Hz (typical 0.56).
  Both are minimum-phase (A-016, now partially verified against S-005's group-delay rows; group delay at 1 kHz 0.09 and 0.05 ms, at 2550 Hz 1.43 and 1.58 ms), implemented as a 5th-order Chebyshev-II low-pass with a 32 dB stopband, plus a 3rd-order Butterworth high-pass at 112.5 Hz in the record stage. The roll-off between 2550 Hz and 3 kHz is a design choice not constrained by any source (reported at 2700 and 2900 Hz: 16 / 33 dB record, 19 / 32 dB playback). Medium confidence: modeled on the datasheet scaled to the measured clock, **not measured against a real unit**. Known deviations, not modeled: the device's partial aliasing of 3000–3450 Hz input into the top of its band, and its DAC imaging residue above 3 kHz; the emulator's rate conversion boundary rejects both by at least 60 dB.
- **Host rates.** 6 kHz is a supported host rate and the identity: with every stage bypassed the output equals the input sample for sample and the latency is 0. 8 and 16 kHz are no longer the identity but 3:4 and 3:8 conversions, so with every stage bypassed their output passes through the rate conversion boundary (flat within ±0.1 dB to 2550 Hz, at least 60 dB rejection from 3000 Hz) instead of being the input itself. **Breaking (0.3 → 0.4):** `SUPPORTED_HOST_RATES` is `[u32; 7]`, `[6000, 8000, 16000, 44100, 48000, 88200, 96000]`; the C API accepts `host_rate_hz = 6000`; any other rate is still rejected. No setting was added or removed.

- Version 0.4.0 (crates and `RR_DR60_VERSION_*`).
- **Default output now drops pauses** (S-001, A-008, A-022 – A-025), so a block can produce fewer output samples than it consumed, or none: read `BlockInfo::produced` (C: `RrDr60BlockInfo.produced`). Through the AGC's static curve (A-017) the default threshold is about −58 dBFS peak input-referred at sensitivity level 3, so the default pauses only on near-silence; an input floor whose peaks exceed about −58 dBFS keeps recording at levels 2–5. To get the 0.2 sound (AGC and band-limiting, fixed length), set `settings.vas.enabled = false` (C: `vas_enabled = false`).
- **Breaking (0.2 → 0.3):** `process` returns `Result<BlockInfo, Error>` and `process_in_place` returns `BlockInfo` (both `#[must_use]`; existing statement-form calls still compile). `rr_dr60_process` takes a new `out_info` parameter, which is always written when not NULL, including on an error status (contracts/c-api.md rule 2 amendment). `RrDr60Settings` grows from 48 to 72 bytes; code built against the 0.2 header must be rebuilt.
- Version 0.3.0 (crates and `RR_DR60_VERSION_*`).
- **Default output now includes the AGC** (A-020). To get the 0.1 sound (band-limiting only), set `settings.agc.enabled = false` (C: `agc_enabled = false`).
- **Breaking (0.1 → 0.2):** `Settings` and the C `RrDr60Settings` no longer implement `Eq`/`Hash`, because the AGC settings are floating-point. `RrDr60Settings` grows from 24 to 48 bytes; `struct_size` must still be at least `sizeof(RrDr60Settings)`, so code built against the 0.1 header must be rebuilt.
- With the AGC on, output can briefly exceed full scale by up to the maximum gain (+40 dB by default, +60 dB at most) after creation, a reset or a long silence. It is never clipped; limit or clip it before converting to integer PCM. Results beyond the `f32` range now saturate to ±`f32::MAX` instead of becoming ±Inf.
- `rr_dr60_detmath` `ln`/`exp` now also run on the processing path (spec 002 R-06); they stay bit-identical and fixed-cost.
- Version 0.2.0 (crates and `RR_DR60_VERSION_*`).

### Removed

- The spec 002 compressor: the analytic-envelope (Hilbert) detector, its 63-tap coefficient table and `tools/filter-design/design_hilbert.py`; the settings `agc.target_dbfs`, `agc.max_gain_db` and `agc.max_attenuation_db` (`Setting::AgcTargetDbfs`, `AgcMaxGainDb`, `AgcMaxAttenuationDb`); the harness configurations `agc_isolated_after_playback` and `agc_tap_stages_on`, the 0.4 AGC golden stimuli `agc_noise_burst`, `agc_start_m10` and `agc_step`, and the reference-ratio measurement method (002 research R-11; the exact per-sample gain is now observable at the "after AGC" tap at every host rate).

### Fixed

- Voice-band biquad state flushing (R-05): the two state values are now flushed together. Flushing them one at a time let the cascade sustain a ~1e-30 limit cycle after loud input, so the tail never reached digital silence. The change affects output only at levels around −600 dBFS.
